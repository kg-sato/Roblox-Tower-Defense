# Music in tower defense: dynamic layers and stage themes (T023)

Question: how do TD games use music (dynamic layers, stage themes), and what should our five stage sets do?

**Gap against the task:** nothing about the Kingdom Rush soundtrack or its composers was found. The composer names in the search query were not confirmed by any result, so none is credited here. The note covers dynamic-music technique only.

## Facts (sourced)
- Vertical layering (vertical orchestration): add or remove layers of one piece to shift intensity, with fades; suits rising tension in a battle. Dead Space 2 has four stereo layers tied to levels of "fear". Horizontal re-sequencing: switch between separate pieces by crossfade, phrase branching (switch after the phrase ends) or a composed bridge; suits major moments such as a location change or a battle start. [Wikipedia, Dynamic music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_music) (fetched 2026-10-28).
- History from the same page: Space Invaders (1978) speeds up its four-note bass as aliens descend; LucasArts iMUSE (debuted in Monkey Island 2, 1991) synchronises music to events; Spore (2008) generates music from gameplay; Ape Out (2019) has a procedural jazz score.
- Search found Ironhide interviews (Ray Wenderlich with co-founder Alvaro Azofra; Pocket Gamer on Kingdom Rush 6: Genesis, 2026) but none covered music. [Ray Wenderlich forum, interview](https://forums.raywenderlich.com/t/making-a-hit-tower-defense-game-a-top-app-dev-interview-ray-wenderlich/46750); [Pocket Gamer, Kingdom Rush 6 dev interview](https://www.pocketgamer.com/kingdom-rush-6-genesis/dev-interview/) (both snippets; not read in full).
- A Bandcamp page titled "Kingdom Rush Soundtrack" lists "Jose Antonini" as artist; it does not say he composed the official score ([Bandcamp](https://joseantonini.bandcamp.com), snippet; treat as unverified).

## Recommendations for us (not sourced rules)
1. Per stage set one horizontal theme (five tracks, T160), crossfaded on set change, since a set change is the kind of moment the page assigns to resequencing.
2. Within a stage, vertical layers: base loop plus tension layers that fade in by wave count (T161) and a boss layer (T186 stings).
3. Duck music under reveals and boss speech (T164).
4. Author each track in 4 to 5 stems that loop on the same grid so Roblox can fade stems by volume; check sound length limits and cost before uploading (uploads are pre-approved, but log ids in VFX/ASSETS.md).
5. Source the Kingdom Rush score by listening, not from this note.
