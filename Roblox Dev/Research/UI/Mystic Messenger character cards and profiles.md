# Mystic Messenger style character cards and profile screens (T037)

Question: what can we take from Mystic Messenger's character presentation for our familiar cards and profile screens?

**Gap against the task:** no source read describes the profile screens' layout, portrait expressions or visual design. The Wikipedia page lists what a character entry contains, as a text description. Everything about card layout below is my recommendation.

## Facts (sourced)
- Mystic Messenger is a South Korean otome ("storytelling messenger") game by Cheritz, released on Android on 8 Jul 2016 and iOS on 18 Aug 2016. Play runs in real time through a messenger: texts and group chats, with chat choices shifting a character's feelings (affection shown as hearts) and the route. A route lasts 11 in-game days. It passed 2.5 million downloads within a year and won Best Indie Game at the 2017 Korea Game Awards. [Wikipedia, Mystic Messenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystic_Messenger) (fetched 2026-10-28).
- Each character entry lists voice actor, age, role and physical traits such as hair and eye colour, followed by personality and relationships. The protagonist's default avatar has five appearances. Same source.
- From search snippets (community wiki, unread because the page returned 402): an open chat room lasts until the next timing opens another; missed rooms can be bought back with in-game currency; after a first ending all chats can be replayed from a Chat History page ([Mystic Messenger wiki, Chatroom Timings](https://mystic-messenger.fandom.com/wiki/Chatroom_Timings)). Time-gated pacing and a replay page are the visible pacing tools.

## Recommendations for our familiar cards (not sourced)
1. Entry fields from the same pattern: name, role, faction, rarity, one-line trait, one-line relationship (who they bond with), then the longer bio on tap (T097, T103). This matches the field list above, adapted to our lore.
2. Show a bond meter in the same way as hearts, linked to the bond system (T207), and let it unlock extra lines.
3. Use expression variants on the portrait for a few moods (idle, pleased, fighting), matching the mood and expression tasks (T110, T208).
4. A short "chat" style pair-banter feed between familiars gives lore in small bites (T068), with a history page to replay seen lines.
5. Avoid real-time-gated content that punishes missed sessions; a missed-room purchase is the kind of mechanic our monetisation note advises against (T189).
