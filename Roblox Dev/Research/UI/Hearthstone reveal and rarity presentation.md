# Hearthstone reveal and rarity presentation (T041)

Question: how does Hearthstone reveal cards and show rarity, and what should our summon reveal take?

All pages fetched 2026-10-28. The community wiki is not an official source; its pity numbers match Blizzard blog snippets only partly (see below).

## Facts (sourced)
- Hearthstone released 11 Mar 2014 (Windows, macOS); mobile in April 2015. Five rarity tiers: Legendary, Epic, Rare, Common, Free. Packs are bought with gold or real money; the designers intended the game to open with the player unboxing cards. Presentation was built around a "watched by an audience" feel: cards sway in hand, slam onto the board; the board shakes on large damage; composers Jason Hayes and Glenn Stafford wrote short stingers that play when Legendary cards are summoned. Gem colours and pity were not covered on this page. [Wikipedia, Hearthstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hearthstone) (first 100,000 of 140,195 characters).
- Pack opening: each pack holds 5 cards shown face down, turned over one at a time by key or click. Hovering shows a border glow matching rarity, based on World of Warcraft gear-quality colours: none for common, blue for rare, purple for epic, orange for legendary. Cards you did not already own get a blue border and a "NEW!" label. The page does not mention rarity gems. [Hearthstone Wiki (wiki.gg), Card packs](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Card_packs) (fan-run wiki; undated).
- Same page: Legendary about 5.0% per card average, guaranteed within 40 standard packs, and one Legendary is guaranteed within the first 10 packs of any type; Epic about 21.0% per card, guaranteed within 10 packs. Duplicate protection: no Legendary copy until you own every Legendary in the set, at most one Legendary copy per pack; no third copy of Common, Rare or Epic until you have two of every card of that rarity in the set.
- Blizzard's own blog (search snippet, unread) describes a flash of gold heralding a Legendary card. [Blizzard, Upcoming card pack changes](https://playhearthstone.com/en-us/blog/20852959)

## Recommendations for our summon reveal (not sourced)
1. Show the result face down first and let the player flip each card (the silhouette first, T057), with a rarity glow on hover or tap; colour plus a shape change per rarity for colour-blind safety.
2. A distinct stinger and a flash for the top rarities only (the Legendary stinger pattern), with the lower rarities kept short so repeated rolls stay quick; skip button always available (T164).
3. Mark new versus owned with a label ("NEW!") so the reveal tells the player something useful.
4. Pity: a displayed counter with a hard guarantee and duplicate protection is standard here, and is the fair model for T200; publish the numbers. Note the pity figures above come from a fan wiki and are Hearthstone's, not ours.
5. Do not copy the orange/purple/blue glow code exactly; they derive from World of Warcraft quality colours and are a common convention, so use our own ladder from the alchemy note.
