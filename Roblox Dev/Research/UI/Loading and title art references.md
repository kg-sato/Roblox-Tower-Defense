# Loading screen and title art references: Hades, Hollow Knight, Bastion (T019)

Question: what do these games do with title and loading presentation that we can reuse?

**Gap against the task:** no read source covers any of the three games' title screen or loading screen directly. What follows is the art and menu presentation that was found. Hollow Knight was not fetched again for this task; see [[Hollow Knight creature design]].

## Facts (sourced)
- Hades: art by in-house artist Jen Zee; no narrator, story delivered through voiced dialogue that changes with past runs; about ten hours of branching dialogue drafted. Announced 6 Dec 2018 with early access that month; full release 17 Sep 2020. [Wikipedia, Hades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_(video_game)) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 118,331 characters read). Title and loading presentation not covered.
- Hades menus have intricate animations by UI and VFX artist kirbyLFO (who has worked on the studio's menus since Transistor); developers treat opening a menu as a chance to pull the player into the world. Art described as vibrant and colourful. [Kotaku, "The Art of Hades"](https://kotaku.com/the-art-of-hades-1845443438) (22 Oct 2020; fetched 2026-10-28). Title screens and key art not covered.
- Bastion (Xbox 360 20 Jul 2011; Windows 16 Aug 2011): Jen Zee's hand-painted look was meant to find beauty in a post-apocalyptic world and soften harsh isometric settings; the narrator (voiced by Logan Cunningham) was added early to give background without long text or cutscenes, speaking in short phrases with long pauses; ground rises under the player as they walk, forming paths. [Wikipedia, Bastion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bastion_(video_game)) (fetched 2026-10-28). The page does not describe a title screen.
- Hollow Knight: signage is minimal and discovery is central (see [[Hollow Knight creature design]]); no title or loading detail read.
- Search for Hades loading and title art found nothing on the title screen or loading tips; the Hades II artwork piece ([Creative Bloq](https://creativebloq.com/art/digital-art/hades-ii-artwork), snippet only, undated) credits Zee for cover art.

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not sourced)
1. Treat the menu as the first scene: animate it as part of the world (Hades' stated approach), which fits the current themed start menu (see the UI folder notes).
2. Fill load time with short narrated or captioned lines (Bastion's short phrases with pauses) rather than a spinner; bracketed caption style in T190.
3. One painted key image for the title screen, not a logo on a flat colour (T060).
4. Let the world assemble on screen (Bastion's rising ground) as a load-in effect for the board.
5. Source the real title and loading screens by playing or watching the games; this note could not.
