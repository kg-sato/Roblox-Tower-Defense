# Fortnite and Dress To Impress: costume and cosmetic presentation (T038)

Question: how do these two games present costumes and cosmetics, and what should our trainer outfits and familiar skins take?

All pages were fetched 2026-10-28. Wikipedia carries little on the visual presentation of cosmetics, so the presentation half (previews, locker layout, rarity colours for cosmetics) is mostly unanswered.

## Facts (sourced)
**Dress To Impress** (Roblox, released 11 Nov 2023; developer Gigi leading the Dress To Impress Group). Players have 360 seconds to build an outfit for a theme (for example back to school, Y2K, formal, album cover) using up to 18 clothing items, plus makeup, skin tone, nail colour and item colours. Outfits are shown on a catwalk where other players rate them one to five stars; the top three appear on a podium; personal ranks unlock items; a no-timer freeplay mode exists. Items are bought with in-game currency or Robux, or limited codes. Limited-time story quests (from Halloween 2024) and collaborations (Charli XCX Brat, August 2024; Lady Gaga, August 2025; Wicked: For Good, November 2025) reward exclusive items. The Brat collaboration peaked at over 651,000 concurrent players. The game won three 2024 Roblox Innovation Awards. [Wikipedia, Dress to Impress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))

**Fortnite Battle Royale.** Cosmetics are outfits, gliders, emotes, backpacks and pickaxes, bought with V-Bucks; the item shop rotates a selection daily; each season's battle pass adds a cosmetic set with free and paid tiers; Fortnite Crew (December 2020) is a monthly subscription with the latest pass, a V-Bucks stipend and exclusive cosmetics. The Wikipedia page lists rarity tiers (Common to Exotic) for weapons, not cosmetics, and gives no colour coding or locker display details. Collaborations include NFL outfits for all 32 teams (November 2018) and Batman comic-code outfits (2021). In March 2026 the battle pass was set at 800 V-Bucks (earlier 1,000). [Wikipedia, Fortnite Battle Royale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortnite_Battle_Royale) and [Wikipedia, Fortnite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortnite) (first 100,000 characters of each read).

## Recommendations for our cosmetics (not sourced)
1. Make the show the point: Dress To Impress frames the cosmetic as a performance on a runway with other players rating it. A similar "parade" scene could show trainer outfits and familiar skins at stage clear (T113, T161), without competitive rating in v1.
2. Constrain the build: a timer, a theme and a cap on items (18 in Dress To Impress) keep outfit choice readable; our trainer variants (four, T113) can be mixed within slots.
3. Keep cosmetics strictly visual (the Fortnite model), with free and paid battle pass tracks, per [[Monetisation players accept vs hate]].
4. Free-play mode: no timer, no pressure, matching accessibility (T024 note).
5. Limited-time collaborations drive attention in both games; given our no-FOMO rule, rerun cosmetics rather than expire them.
6. Study presentation by recording a play session of both games and noting the preview, then update this note.
