# Skill tree R2 review

Verdict: 5/10, FAIL.

Caveat: both reference JPGs (images 1-2) were blocked by the overnight Read hook (path outside project). I did not work around it. Reference comparison is from general knowledge of pro skill trees; images 3-5 were read and graded.

## Scores
- Shape/silhouette: 5. Nodes sit on a near-regular ring around the hub. Branches curve a little, but there are no bold S-curves and no irregular clusters or sub-branches.
- Icon quality: 5. Thin generic line glyphs, small in the discs, similar weight, low contrast on locked nodes. Reads as an icon-font set, not hand-made art.
- Colour/lighting: 5. Gold on black is right. Off-palette: navy-blue sky and vignette, blue-white tree art, purple floor blobs, violet vertical beams, lavender orbs, grey-purple FAMILIARS button, white-bordered X.
- Tarot/arcane feel: 6. Roman numeral cards and the zodiac tick ring work. Cards are tiny, and they collide with nodes and the popup.
- Readability: 5. Owned (cream) is clear. Available vs locked is only a faint ring difference. Popup text is small and "2 skill points" has a tiny "2". The popup covers neighbouring nodes. RESPEC is nearly invisible.
- Polish: 5. Stray beams, clipped card I, card VI overlapping a node, "2/5" badge clashes with the popup.

## Can I tell ours from the references?
Likely yes, at a glance. Ours is a flat ring of equal discs with thin lines. Pro trees have varied node sizes, dense branching, rich lit art.

## Three biggest differences
1. Layout is uniform and radial, not organic.
2. Palette leaks blue/purple and the centre art is photo-like.
3. Icons and chrome (cards, buttons, X, popup) are small and thin, with weak state contrast.

## Three fixes
1. Hand-place nodes irregularly. Vary node sizes (major/minor). Use bold S-curve gold-ink branches with a few forks.
2. Retint the whole scene to black/gold/cream: remove blue sky, purple blobs and beams, and desaturate the tree to gold sepia.
3. Make states unmistakable: locked is dark and desaturated with no glow, available has a pulsing gold ring, owned is solid cream. Enlarge icons to fill 60% of the disc with a thicker gold-ink stroke. Move the popup beside the node. Restyle RESPEC, FAMILIARS and X as gold-framed tarot plates.
