# Title r3_edit_c review

Scores /10: awe/scale 6, silhouette/design 4, colour/lighting 6, menu 7, polish 4. Verdict: FAIL.

Biggest issues
1. Entity reads as a hooded reaper/wraith, not a deity. Black circle face is a flat pasted hole. Outline is a hard, jagged glow halo with a visible cut-out edge, especially at the hood and sleeves. It looks like a sticker over the scene.
2. Entity is not integrated. Its lower body is cut off at the bridge, with no grounding, no shadow, no atmosphere between it and the city. Its texture is sharper and lighter than the muddy background, so the layers mismatch. The player and lantern are a tiny speck, so the scale relation and the facing read weakly.
3. Light is weak. The rays are faint and the "luminous source" is only a dim haze behind the hood. The title and button are fine, but the button is a flat dark box that blends in, with no hover or border treatment. The right and bottom are muddy.

Fixes
1. Replace the cutout edge: feather the matte and add a rim-light gradient. Swap the black disc for a soft void with a crown, halo or antlers to push deity over reaper.
2. Add ground mist, a cast shadow and a colour grade over the whole entity. Add depth haze. Lower the entity's contrast to match the scene and brighten the player lantern.
3. Make a real focal sun, a bright core behind the head with stronger god-rays. Give the button a gold border and fill, then darken the mid-right background.
