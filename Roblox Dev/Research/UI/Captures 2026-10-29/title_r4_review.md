Verdict: FAIL (default; borderline on art, fails on brief).

Top issues

2. Reaper silhouette with black void face and layer-cut edges.
3. Flat mid-value body, weak contrast against background; bridge not crisp.

Fixes

2. Replace face void with a bright halo/crown or geometric mandala; add symmetrical ornament, remove smoke tatters; feather or mask the right/bottom edges.
