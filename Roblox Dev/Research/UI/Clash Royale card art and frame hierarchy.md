# Clash Royale card art and card frame hierarchy (T039)

Question: how does Clash Royale build a readable card hierarchy (rarity, cost, art), and what should our familiar cards take?

**Gap against the task:** no readable source describes the frame colours, frame shapes or card layout. The text sources give rarity tiers, cost as a number and the art style only. Frame hierarchy is unanswered; the layout ideas below are my recommendations from the facts.

## Facts (sourced)
- Released worldwide 2 Mar 2016 on iOS, Android and (Oct 2023) Windows; soft launch 4 Jan 2016 (iOS). Rarities: Common, Rare and Epic at soft launch; Legendary added at global launch; Champions (activatable abilities) in October 2021; Heroes in December 2025. Decks hold eight cards with four in hand; cards level up by collecting copies and paying currency. Elixir generates passively up to 10 and doubles in the final minute. Chest timers were removed in April 2025 for gacha-style "lucky drops". Art is cartoony and colourful; characters were adapted from Clash of Clans for a vertical phone format; Metacritic 86/100; the "pay to win" debate is noted. [Wikipedia, Clash Royale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clash_Royale) (fetched 2026-10-28).
- A 2018 card guide shows each card as an art image with a rarity label and a numeric elixir cost, and labels types such as Ground Troop and Spell; the text does not describe rarity colours or art. [iMore, Clash Royale card guide](https://www.imore.com/clash-royale-card-guide) (last updated 24 Apr 2018; fetched 2026-10-28).
- A search snippet says rarity is marked by a coloured border, without saying which colour is which, and a community wiki page was unreadable (402). ([Clash Royale Wiki](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Card_Rarity), unread.)

## Recommendations for our familiar cards (not sourced)
1. Rarity ladder in the frame: use the alchemical stage ladder (black, white, yellow, red, with a many-colour finish) from [[Occult and alchemy symbol language]], and add a frame shape change at each rung (notches, corner flourishes) so it reads without colour (see [[Colour theory for readable teams]]).
2. Put cost or level in one fixed corner as a number; the player's eye should always find it in the same place (T095 detail panel).
3. Card art: silhouette on a flat tinted background so it reads at hand size, with the name strip below; the full art stays on the detail panel (T096).
4. Cap the number of ornaments per frame; the lower rarities stay plain so the top rung is rare to the eye.
5. Check the final cards at the size they appear in the HUD slot and in the collection grid (T058, T137).
6. Confirm the real frame look by viewing the game before copying anything, and do not reproduce its frames.
