# Loading, start menu and skill tree: blind grading (2026-10-28)

Method: [[Harsh Grading]]. A fresh reviewer per screen, per round, saw only the user's reference and our frames + motion sheet, unlabelled (packets in `grade/`, `grade2/`, `grade3/`). Any item under 7 fails. All three reviewers picked out ours with high confidence every round.

| Screen | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Loading | shape 7, texture 7, colour 6, motion 4, readability 5, request 5 | 7, 7, 6, 4, 5, 5 | 6, 5, 6, 4, 5, 4 | **FAIL by reviewer** |
| Start menu | layout 5, Roblox fit 8, type 5, colour 6, polish 4 | 6, 8, 6, 6, 5 | 6, 8, 6, 6, 5 | **FAIL by reviewer** |
| Skill tree | icons 3, border 4, popup 5, layout 4, originality 6 | 5, 6, 4, 6, 6 | 5, 4, 7, 6, 7 | **FAIL by reviewer** |

Fixed between rounds: loading glitch bars and title band removed, eyes re-cut to melt into the dark, a real swallow (core of light + spiral arcs) instead of a black gap, cream tips; menu on one left edge, 18 px body, crisp lamp label, light pillar hidden, flash switch in the button face; tree icons bigger, light runners, flickering lanterns, drifting motes, stray outer hexes removed. After round 2 (not regraded): gazette line no longer cut off, gear moved off the watch crown.

## Round 3 (the user said "do the fixes")
Built: skill nodes are small gas lanterns (unlit iron / brass ready to kindle / lit glass that flickers), bolder glyphs, a light flat brass popup with a pocket-watch crest placed beside the tree (never over it); loading tunnel speed lines and motion-blur copies, a spiral-of-streaks swallow, no pixel squares, eyes open through a soft slit; the featured familiar shaded (low ambient, warm key, neon to metal). After round 3 (not regraded): the tree's slice seam fixed, gears turn steadily, dimmer centre flare.
Reviewer notes from round 3: stills at 0.5 s steps miss the growth and eye opening; eyes still read as one cloned engraving; generic glyphs; the menu's thin 1 px frames read web-like; the title is not inky enough for them.

## Round 4 (2026-10-28 night, the user's feedback list)
Built: see the Session Log line. Reviewer scores: loading + tour 6/6/7/4/7/5; tree + grove 5/5/7/5/4/7; menus + roll (settings 5, progression 5, currencies 6, summon 4). All **FAIL by reviewer**.
Fixed after round 4 (not regraded): tree screen banding (a gradient rendered as hard bands), path tails off, bigger and brighter glyphs; grove's grey beam, white disc and horizon line removed; currency names 14 px; summon card wait 2.5 s to 1.2 s; the summon's yellow dots (the reviewer's frames were from before that fix).
Reviewer limits: stills at 1 s steps; it judged the tour as cuts and the eyes as still, which the 20 fps recording does not show (the tour glides; the irises turn and blink), but both motions are subtle.
Still open in their words: a 2D tree image (wants real 3D or layered), clean title font rather than inky, settings and progression panels plain inside, summon familiar models are primitive.

## What still fails (rounds 1-2) (reviewers' words, condensed)
- **Loading:** the tunnel does not feel like falling (needs continuous travel and blur); the vortex reads as a tilted crossfade; the eyes are engraved clip-art beside the reference's photoreal eyes; the title glow is soft rather than inky.
- **Start menu:** the featured Star Drake renders as flat yellow (the pet model's neon look, not this screen's code); four typefaces; lower third empty; the lamp button is a plain thin frame.
- **Skill tree:** glyph icons are small and generic, and the nodes are not drawn as lanterns; the clockwork popup frame is photoreal and heavy next to the flat tree; secondary popup text small.
