## 1. Tarot card rolling (the user's model to build off)
**Main pin `tarot_main/…405377` ("Black Gold Tarot Cards, animated stream alerts", 10 s video).** Both design and animation, per the user.
- Palette measured on the sampled frames: near-black #111010 to #23201e, dark olive-gold #423a26, warm gold #766c52, with a gold glow vignette in one corner; cards are grey-silver with black-and-white line art; gold only on sparkles, rays and the corner ornament.
- Sequence (about 0.42 s per sample): a small tilted card arrives with 8 short ray ticks and a tiny ring (0 to 0.4 s), turns to show its back (a crescent moon, 0.4 to 1.2 s), two thin circles expand beside it; at about 1.7 s the card bursts to white with sparkle particles, then the face appears (a moth with a black ribbon banner behind it) with radial rays and four-point stars for about 0.8 s; the burst settles into a fan of cards behind the hero card with a banner beneath; a second hero card (a cauldron) repeats the same reveal in the lower half; the loop ends with a card edge-on flip (about 9.6 s).
- Takeaways: (1) card back first, then a white burst, then the face (a three-beat reveal); (2) rays plus four-point stars plus tiny rings are the whole VFX vocabulary, kept small; (3) the fan of other cards behind the winner shows "what you did not get"; (4) a banner ribbon under the card holds the name. All of this maps onto our The Reading reveal; it is a design check, not a replacement.
- Unclear: card size ratio and exact timing per beat; samples are coarse.

## 3. Skill tree (the user: "too straight / clunky; more whimsical, less straightforward")
`skilltree/…834754` (a hub and spokes talent web, "Intrinsic Persona Web": icons on short curved lines around a centre), `…484736` (Mandragora Witch Tree: a dense hand-drawn branching network with a glowing core), `…514329` (Identity V: nodes as glass-ring buttons on dotted organic paths over a nebula; the side panel is a big ring with a number), `…373094` (a data-viz tree with a long fan of thin curves from one node), `…467273` (a radial timeline with fine ticks), `…544000` (a neural-network style poster: thousands of thin coloured curves branching from a few stems).
Takeaways: (1) branches are curves with a gentle S shape, not straight segments or right angles; (2) nodes are not on a grid: spacing varies, clusters sit near the stem; (3) a thick stem with many hairline branches reads as growth ("the tree grows") and gives an animation: draw the line from the stem outward when a node unlocks; (4) a centre hub with a glow keeps orientation; (5) locked nodes are dim ring outlines, unlocked ones are lit and slightly larger. Concrete targets: ring buttons of about 56 to 72 px, branch curve stroke 2 to 3 px, glow only on unlocked paths.
