# Bloodborne lantern and fog design: light as gameplay and mood (T036)

Question: how does Bloodborne use lamps and fog as gameplay and mood, and what should our Lantern theme take?

**Gap against the task:** the sources read do not cover fog or lighting at all. They cover lamps as checkpoints and the city's mood. Fog walls (a Dark Souls feature) were also not found in the Dark Souls page I read. The fog half of the question is unanswered.

## Facts (sourced)
- Bloodborne (PlayStation 4, released 24 Mar 2015 in North America): lamps scattered through Yharnam act as checkpoints and let the player return to the Hunter's Dream hub, where items are bought and weapons upgraded. Yharnam is a decaying Gothic city, partly inspired by Dracula and by architecture in Romania and the Czech Republic; critics praised the atmosphere (Game Informer: the visuals "had brought horror to life"). Defeating Rom reveals the blood moon. [Wikipedia, Bloodborne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloodborne) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 109,821 characters). The page does not mention fog or lighting.
- Dark Souls bonfires are the series' checkpoints; resting restores health and resources but respawns most enemies, so repeated returns cost progress. Vice called the bonfire a "mark of genius" that "reinvented the save point". [Wikipedia, Dark Souls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Souls) (fetched 2026-10-28). Nothing on fog gates.
- From search snippets (fan wiki and a level design article, unread): Bloodborne lamps are small streetlight-style structures that must be lit to activate, described as the spiritual successor to bonfires; Central Yharnam is cited as the place where the level design teaches the most ([Bloodborne fandom wiki, Awakening Headstone](https://bloodborne.fandom.com/wiki/Awakening_Headstone), snippet; [Wikipedia, Yharnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yharnam), listing). The A.V. Club's Julie Muncy is quoted praising the first half's "meticulously crafted geography" (snippet).

## Recommendations for our Lantern theme (not sourced)
1. Light as checkpoint: a lit lamp on the board marks a safe restart or a rewarding point, so the Lantern visually means safety (T011 Lantern power, T181 exit gate).
2. Light as progress: lamps relight as stages are cleared, so the city's mood moves from fog to warm light, tied to the cosmic turn (T054).
3. Fog as boundary: use fog to hide the next lane or the boss arena edge (boss arena T172), and clear it when the telegraph fires, so the fog is both mood and information.
4. Keep the lamp as a repeated, easily recognised shape (one glow style, T144), brightest at decision points.
5. Performance check for fog and lights on phones before committing (see [[Mobile performance limits]]).
