# Brawl Stars: brawler cards (T009)

Question: how are Brawl Stars brawlers framed by name, role, rarity, lore length and art style, and what should our familiar cards take?

## Facts (sourced)
- Worldwide release 12 Dec 2018 (iOS, Android) after a 522-day soft launch starting June 2017 in Canada. Setting: the abandoned Starr Park amusement park. The game moved from 2D to 3D during development. [Wikipedia, Brawl Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brawl_Stars) (fetched 2026-10-28).
- Seven classes: Damage Dealer, Marksman, Tank, Assassin, Support, Controller, Artillery. Same source. A guide site lists six (no Artillery) and says some brawlers fit two classes; seven is the fuller count. [Guildorder, class overview](https://guildorder.com/games/brawl_stars/wiki/brawler-classes-overview) (search summary, undated).
- Six rarity tiers: Rare, Super Rare, Epic, Mythic, Legendary, Ultra Legendary. The page says rarity reflects how hard a brawler's concept is to design. Same Wikipedia source.
- Each brawler has an attack, a Super (charged by damage), a Gadget and Hypercharge (activated), and passive Star Power; Gears are not brawler-specific. Same source.
- Unlocks: Starr Road replaced boxes in Dec 2022; Rare and Super Rare unlock via Trophy Road; Brawl Pass has a free track; the page says gems mostly come from real-money purchases. A "Brawler Blast" system replaced Starr Road in Sep 2026 (page statement, unchecked). Same source.
- Role descriptions (tanks absorb, assassins dive, artillery breaks cover) are from guide sites, search summaries only: [MetaForge, tank ranking](https://metaforge.app/brawlstars/tank-brawler-ranked-which-one-fits-your-playstyle), [Pocket Tactics, all brawlers](https://www.pockettactics.com/brawl-stars/brawlers) (about 100 brawlers in 2026).

## Not found
- Backstory length per card and art-style rules: no source read states either. The Wikipedia page does not describe the art style.

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not facts)
1. Cards need only: name, class, rarity, and one ability line; the numbers above show kit depth lives in abilities, not text.
2. A small fixed class set (we could use 5 to 7) keeps the roster readable; allow dual-class sparingly.
3. Define rarity as design complexity (more moving parts), not just stronger numbers (T099, T192).
4. Keep lore to one line on the card and put the long bio in the collection (T097).
5. Our "no pay-to-win" plan (T189) differs from gem-driven unlocks; take only the free track idea.
