# Roll and gacha reveal pacing in games that are not predatory (T016)

Question: how do roll and reveal systems differ between predatory and fair designs, with Brawl Stars and Pokemon Go as references?

## Facts (sourced)
- Common loot-box and gacha reveal tricks: slot-machine-style reveals, rarity colour-coding before the reveal, "near miss" reveals where a rare item shows briefly before a lesser one lands, limited-time banners, urgency timers, streak rewards. Critics call these dark patterns. [Wikipedia, Loot box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loot_box) and [Wikipedia, Gacha game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gacha_game) (both fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 characters of Loot box read).
- Pity types: soft pity (odds rise after a dry run), hard pity (guaranteed at a fixed count, which caps the cost), sparking (currency per pull, exchange for a chosen item), and 50/50 systems. Same Gacha game page.
- Regulation: China required odds disclosure from May 2017 (announced Dec 2016); Belgium ruled loot boxes gambling in April 2018; the Netherlands ruled against boxes with transferable items in April 2018 (EA's FIFA fine later overturned in March 2022); South Korea enforces published odds from March 2024; Japan banned "complete gacha" in 2012. Same two pages.
- Brawl Stars removed Brawl Boxes in the 12 Dec 2022 update, replaced by Starr Road: Credits earned through Brawl Pass, Trophy Road or the shop unlock Brawlers, with visible progress per Brawler. Supercell said this is "more fair and predictable" with clearer goals; the effect on earnings was unclear. [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2022/12/13/brawl-stars-loot-boxes-removed-supercell) (13 Dec 2022; fetched 2026-10-28). The Wikipedia Brawl Stars page (read earlier, see [[Brawl Stars brawler cards]]) says a "Brawler Blast" random unlock replaced Starr Road in Sep 2026, and a search summary says boxes returned later ([Mobile Matters](https://mobilematters.gg/news/brawl-stars/brawl-stars-boxes-return), snippet only, undated). So "Brawl Stars is fair" is not a stable claim.
- Pokemon Go: no battle for wild creatures; the player throws a ball with success depending on catch rate, timing and ball type; catches award Candy and Stardust; Community Day (from 20 Jan 2018) raises Shiny odds for a featured creature; Buddy Pokemon earn Candy by walking. [Wikipedia, Pokemon Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Go) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 236,776 characters). The page describes no pity system or reveal animation.

## Not found
- No source on reveal animation timing (seconds) for either game; the "pacing" half of the question is unanswered. Pokemon Go is skill-influenced, not a paid-pull gacha, so it is only a loose comparison.

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not sourced)
1. Publish odds in the roll screen and keep the pity rule visible (T200, T030 odds panel already exists).
2. Prefer hard pity with a displayed counter (it caps cost); avoid near-miss fakes and urgency timers (T240).
3. Use rarity colour-coding in the reveal only after the silhouette is shown, so the lore and look lead (T057).
4. Offer a visible track toward a chosen familiar as Starr Road did, alongside random rolls.
5. Treat reveal duration as untested: set a short default with skip (T164 ducking, T159 reveal sounds) and measure in playtest.
