# Pokemon-style creature archetypes as merge units (T007)

Question: which Pokemon design habits (role, colour, lore hook, evolution logic) suit merge units, and what should our familiars take?

## Facts (sourced)
- Red and Green launched 27 Feb 1996 with 151 species; about 1,025 species by early 2026. [Wikipedia, Pokemon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 238,313 characters read).
- Ken Sugimori led character design; Atsuko Nishida created Pikachu. The Pokedex was proposed as a portable encyclopedia for tracking catches. The starter choice is a trio of three types. Same source. The page excerpt did not cover the 18 types or silhouette rules.
- Masuda (Game Freak) says designers treat each Pokemon as a living creature: imagine its habitat, diet and why it looks as it does; a design needs an in-world reason. Evolutions are sometimes driven by simple ideas like extra body parts, and the team asks why each change happens. [Siliconera, "Game Freak discusses Pokemon designs, approach to evolutions"](https://www.siliconera.com/game-freak-discusses-pokmon-designs-approach-evolutions/) (11 Aug 2017; fetched 2026-10-28).
- Nishida's Pikachu brief was an "electric type" with two evolutions, so the evolution line was part of the design brief. [GoNintendo summary of the Pokemon Company interview](https://gonintendo.com/stories/310443-game-freak-goes-into-great-detail-on-the-creation-of-pikachu) (search summary, undated, unread).
- Colour as a type signal and the silhouette test appear only in fan and blog pages; not documented Game Freak doctrine. [Smogon, Pokemon art through the ages](https://www.smogon.com/articles/pokemon-art-through-ages) (listing only, unread).

## Not found
- No primary source on silhouette rules or per-type colour policy.

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not facts)
1. Brief a merge unit as role plus element plus an evolution line from the start; our 3 tiers per familiar (T100) should each add one visible body feature with a reason.
2. Give each familiar a "why it looks like this" line tied to its habitat in Lampwick Lane (T074, T097).
3. Use a starter-style choice of three at the first roll for a clear first role.
4. A collection log like the Pokedex gives the lore card home (T097, T103).
5. Use colour to mark element, but verify with the colour-blind test (T088) since no source supports colour alone.
