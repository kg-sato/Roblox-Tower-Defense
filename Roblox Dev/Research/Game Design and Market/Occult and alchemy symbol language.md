# Occult and alchemy iconography as a symbol language (T013)

Question: which real alchemical symbols and systems can give the game a consistent symbol language for rarity, elements, factions and status?

## Facts (sourced)
- Seven metals pair with seven classical planets: silver Moon, gold Sun, quicksilver Mercury, copper Venus, iron Mars, tin Jupiter, lead Saturn. Western alchemy also has glyphs for air, earth, fire, water. Paracelsus (1493 to 1541) named three primes: sulfur, mercury, salt. [Wikipedia, Alchemical symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alchemical_symbol) (fetched 2026-10-28).
- Notation was never standardised; one inventory lists 3,695 symbols and variants; symbols were used until the 18th century (Basil Valentine table 1670; Bergman 1775). Unicode has an Alchemical Symbols block since Unicode 6.0 (2010), range U+1F700. Same source.
- Ouroboros: a serpent or dragon biting its tail, meaning cycles of renewal; earliest known in the Egyptian Enigmatic Book of the Netherworld (14th century BCE, Tutankhamun's tomb); alchemical use in the Chrysopoeia of Cleopatra ("the all is one", text probably 3rd century Alexandria, surviving drawing from a 10th-century copy); a 1625 engraving uses a wyvern form for mercury. Variants include winged and black-and-white halves. [Wikipedia, Ouroboros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouroboros) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 207,825 characters read).
- The Great Work has colour stages: nigredo (black), albedo (white), citrinitas (yellow; often merged into the next stage after the 15th century), rubedo (red or purple). Cauda pavonis ("peacock's tail", many colours) is an intermediate phase. [Wikipedia, Magnum opus (alchemy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnum_opus_(alchemy)) (fetched 2026-10-28).

## Symbol language for the game (recommendations, not sourced facts)
1. **Rarity ladder from the Great Work stages:** black, white, yellow, red as common to epic, and a many-colour "peacock" finish for mythic. It is real, ordered, and distinct from the generic grey-green-blue-purple ladder. Check against the colour-blind test (T088).
2. **Elements:** keep the four classical glyphs as faction/element badges; the planetary metals can tag the seven material recipes in T105 (brass is copper and zinc, so copper-Venus fits Foundry units).
3. **Ouroboros** as the merge/evolve symbol: a ring that closes when a merge tier completes (T100, T096). It also fits "the clock stopped" lore (T059).
4. **Free use of glyphs:** Unicode 6.0 symbols are text characters, but check that the Roblox font renders them; if not, draw them as icons (T081).
5. Do not copy one author's table; notation varied, so we define our own consistent set and write it in the glossary (T058).
