# Genshin Impact: character kits and element identity (T006)

Question: how does Genshin tie each character to an element, a kit and a region, and what should our familiars take from it?

## Facts (sourced)
- Released 28 Sep 2020 (Android, iOS, PS4, Windows). Seven elements: Anemo, Geo, Pyro, Hydro, Cryo, Electro, Dendro. Elements react when they meet (Hydro then Cryo freezes; Pyro with Electro overloads). [Wikipedia, Genshin Impact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genshin_Impact) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 200,304 characters read).
- Each character has two signature abilities: an Elemental Skill (cooldown) and an Elemental Burst (needs energy). Parties of four swap in combat to combine elements. Same source.
- Most characters come from gacha "Wishes" with a pity system that guarantees a rare after a set number of pulls. Same source.
- Every character is tied to one element and one region; regional culture drives design. The roster is grouped by region and faction. The page reports 120 playable characters as of Sep 2026. [Wikipedia, List of Genshin Impact characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Genshin_Impact_characters) (fetched 2026-10-28; first half read).
- Same page: development starts from a background story and home location, then art style, background and gameplay; characters use cel shading; a "Vision" device channels the element.
- Third-party guides claim silhouettes should read at a distance, and that colour scheme echoes element and home nation. No HoYoverse designer source was found; treat as commentary. [XP-Pen, Genshin Character Design: Overview](https://www.xp-pen.com/blog/genshin-character-designs.html) (search summary, undated, unread).

## Not found
- No official designer interview. The silhouette and colour rules above are third-party opinion.

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not facts)
1. Fix a small element set (we have ember, frost, stone, glass, star-dust, ink, brass) and tie each to a faction (T053) and a colour (T088).
2. Two signature moves per familiar (an attack style and an ultimate) matches our ultimate effects (T138); keep each a single sentence.
3. Reactions between elements are a cheap source of synergy data (T193, T194); keep them in a data table.
4. Pity is standard in gacha; keep ours documented and tested (T200).
5. Start every familiar from a home place and a story hook, then art, then mechanics (T074, T056).
