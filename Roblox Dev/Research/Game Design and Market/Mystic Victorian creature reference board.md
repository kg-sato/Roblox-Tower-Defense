## Gothic (shape and ornament)
Source: [Wikipedia, Gothic Revival architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_Revival_architecture) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 329,865 characters read).
- Lancet (narrow pointed) windows, pointed arches, rose windows, trefoil openings, blind arcading.
- Tracery (lacelike openwork), fretwork panels, niches, finials, hood moulds.
- Steep roofs and gables, turrets, battlements.
- Era: precursors from Strawberry Hill (1749); mainstream in the early 1800s; peak about the 1870s; decline in the 1880s and early 1890s; Palace of Westminster built 1840 to 1876.
- Use: a pointed-arch silhouette plus tracery and finials reads Gothic at once (the page's own tip; applies to familiar crowns, horns and cathedral props).

## Clockwork (material and parts)
Source: [Wikipedia, Steampunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steampunk) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 319,384 characters read).
- Polished brass, iron, wood and leather; exposed cogs and timepieces; goggles, pocket watches, parasols; Victorian dress such as top hats, waistcoats and tailcoats.
- The term was coined by K. W. Jeter in a letter in Locus, April 1987. Disney's 20,000 Leagues Under the Sea (1954) and Albert Robida's late-1800s prints shaped later looks.
- Use: Brass Hound, Clockwork Owl and Sentinel (T079, T082, T084) take brass plating and visible gears; keep the gear count low enough for the 20,000 triangle limit.

## Occult (light and presence)
Source: [Wikipedia, Spirit photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirit_photography) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 116,097 characters read).
- Spirit photography: glass-plate reuse from about 1859; William Mumler's widely publicised case in 1862 (Boston); declined in the 1920s after exposures by skeptics such as Houdini.
- Visual conventions: translucent figures, double exposure, ethereal blur from long exposure.
- Context: Spiritualism grew in the mid-1800s with grief after the American Civil War.
- Use: the Mourner and Grey Veil Wraith (T083, T086) can use translucent, doubled edges and soft blur instead of hard outlines.
