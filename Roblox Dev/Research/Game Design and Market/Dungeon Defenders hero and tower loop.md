# Dungeon Defenders: hero and tower loop, and what makes a roster feel full (T008)

Question: how does Dungeon Defenders combine a hero with defenses, and what makes its roster feel full?

## Facts (sourced)
- Release: iOS 16 Dec 2010, Android 23 Dec 2010, PS3 18 Oct 2011 (NA), Windows and Xbox Live Arcade 19 Oct 2011, Mac 15 Mar 2012, Linux 19 Dec 2012. Metacritic 81 (PC), 80 (PS3), 77 (Xbox 360), 66 (iOS). Over 1,000,000 sold by Feb 2012. [Wikipedia, Dungeon Defenders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Defenders) (fetched 2026-10-28).
- Four classes shipped; eight more came as paid DLC on Windows, for twelve total. Eight of the twelve have their own defenses and weapons; the other four are gender counterparts of the originals. Every class has at least two special abilities. Same source.
- Heroes build and upgrade defenses and also fight directly. Defenses are limited by mana and a per-level cap on defense units. Same source.
- Loop: roughly five waves per level, some ending in a boss; a long prep time before each wave on easier difficulty; lose the level if the Eternia Crystal takes too much damage. Same source.
- Mana comes from kills and chests between waves, and banks into gear upgrades. Pets add damage and count for kill bonuses. Co-op for 1 to 4 players, sometimes 6. Same source.
- Search summaries describe the wave as a Build phase then a Combat phase where players can still build but enemies interrupt them ([Push Square review](https://www.pushsquare.com/reviews/2011/10/dungeon_defenders_psn), [GodisaGeek review](https://www.godisageek.com/2011/11/dungeon-defenders-review); both unread, snippet only).

## Not found

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not facts)
1. A roster feels full when each member has its own toolkit, not just stats: here eight of twelve have unique defenses and weapons. Our familiars should each own a distinct attack style (T133, T196).
2. Reskinned counterparts (four of twelve) count toward the headline number but add little; avoid recolour clones (T099 says rarity changes the silhouette).
3. Two special abilities per class is a good floor for our familiar kit (T138).
4. A per-level cap on defense units and a banked-mana economy mirror our slot limit and merge economy (T185, T190).
5. Build-then-combat phases fit our waves; ties to the wave pause setting (T006 pause).
