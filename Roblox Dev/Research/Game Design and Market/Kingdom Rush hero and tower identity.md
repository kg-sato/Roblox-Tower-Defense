# Kingdom Rush: hero and tower identity (T002)

Question: how does Kingdom Rush give each tower and hero a distinct silhouette, ability and role, and what should our familiars take from it?

## Facts (sourced)
- Original game has four tower types: archers, mages, artillery, barracks. Artillery is best against groups, archers hit flyers, barracks soldiers block and slow enemies. Source: [Wikipedia, Kingdom Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Rush) (fetched 2026-10-28).
- Each tower takes three upgrades, then one of two final branches with different abilities (a mage tower becomes Arcane Wizard or Sorcerer). Same source.
- Vengeance (22 Nov 2018) drops the two-way final upgrade and offers 16 tower types, 5 per level, so any tower can cover another's role. Same source; also [MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/game/54588) (search summary, not fetched).
- One hero is player-controlled and free-moving; heroes unlock by clearing levels, each with its own abilities. Alliance (25 Jul 2024) lets the player control two heroes. Wikipedia as above.
- Two level-wide abilities recharge over time: area meteor and reinforcements. Wikipedia as above.
- Early games: each hero has two special abilities that grow per level; later games use level-up points. [TV Tropes, Kingdom Rush](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/KingdomRush) (search summary; page returned 403 to fetch; community page, lower reliability).
- Art: Gonzalo Sande aimed for a detailed, cartoony style; critics praised the lighthearted look (IGN 9.0/10, Metacritic iOS 89). Wikipedia as above.
- Hero roster size varies by source (13 to 14 per game, Origins 16): unverified, see [Kingdom Rush Wiki, Heroes](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Heroes) (returned 402; not read).

## Not found
- No Ironhide postmortem or designer talk on silhouette rules turned up. Any claim that Kingdom Rush "uses" a silhouette rule is inference from the roles above, not a sourced statement.

## Design takeaways for us (recommendations, not facts)
1. One role per base unit, stated in one word (block, burst, reach, support); the role should be visible from shape, so give each familiar family a distinct silhouette (ties to T087).
2. Three upgrades then a fork gives a clear identity moment; Vengeance shows the cost of removing it (roles blur). Keep forks for our upgrade paths (T196).
3. A free-moving hero plus recharging global abilities maps to our Lantern power (T011).
4. Keep faction identity in the palette and prop language so a new player reads roles at a glance.
