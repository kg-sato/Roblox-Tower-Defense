# Merge-game progression and what keeps players for 30 days (T015)

Question: what do Merge Mansion and similar merge games do for progression curves and 30-day retention?

## Facts (sourced)
- Merge Mansion: iOS and Android, September 2020, by Metacore (its first game). Merge two items to get an upgraded one (two knives become pruning shears); merging renovates the mansion, opens areas and advances a story. Over 40 million downloads by 16 Aug 2021; $38.6 million from 1 Jan to 16 Nov 2021. [Wikipedia, Merge Mansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merge_Mansion) (fetched 2026-10-28). The page has no retention or energy data.
- Supercell's developer relations lead Jaakko Harlas called Merge Mansion's 24% day-30 retention strong, the level seen with a hit. [PocketGamer.biz, Metacore secures $29.6M from Supercell](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/metacore-secures-296-million-from-supercell-as-it-rebrands-from-everywear-games/) (17 Sep 2020; fetched 2026-10-28). The figure is a statement in a news article, not published data.
- Voodoo's Plantopia targeted 15% day-30 retention and benchmarked its loop against Merge Mansion. Design notes from that interview: Merge Mansion uses one board, which gets cramped and forces selling items; Plantopia unlocks boards gradually (one for the first 20 minutes, a second soon after, a third around day 3); it has no energy system; customer requests are personal; Merge Mansion's meta is mostly cosmetic and weakly tied to merging, so Plantopia ties meta progress to core output. [Elite Game Developers, EGD News #85](https://elitegamedevelopers.substack.com/p/egd-news-85-designing-a-merge-game) (4 Jun 2021; fetched 2026-10-28). This is a competitor's account of a rival, not Metacore's own.
- Another merge game, Gossip Harbor, is described as running about 20 events a month of 1 to 10 days each for retention. [Mobidictum, Gossip Harbor liveops](https://mobidictum.com/gossip-harbors-liveops/) (search summary only, undated).

## Not found
- Merge Dragons: the Wikipedia page returned 404; no data on it was read.
- No independent day-30 curve, and nothing on what specifically drives retention in Merge Mansion. The links from board count to retention are inference.

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not sourced)
1. Reasonable bars: about 15% to 24% day-30 retention is the range quoted for strong merge titles; set our own target after the playtest (T239).
2. Introduce the second surface early, as Plantopia does with boards, so the player sees there is more than one thing to do (our roster, collection and skill tree).
3. Tie meta progress to core output (our skill tree and collection fed by merge results), not cosmetics alone.
4. A story hook between merges carries the loop in Merge Mansion; our lore lines per stage (T062 to T067) do the same job.
5. Events: frequent short events cost live-ops effort; at solo scale, plan a small number (T017, T022) and avoid fake countdowns (T240).
