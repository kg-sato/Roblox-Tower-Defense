# How strong Roblox TD games handle unit progression, merge and evolve (T014)

Question: what do the biggest Roblox tower defense games do for merge and evolve loops?

**Gap against the task:** a ranked "top 10 by visits" list could not be built. The Roblox games API returned 404, one list site returned 402 and one 403. Seven games from one list are covered below; none was confirmed to have a merge or fuse mechanic. Evolve is stated for two of them.

## What each game does (from one list article)
Source: [Switchblade Gaming, Best Roblox Tower Defense Games](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/roblox/best-tower-defense-games/) (page shows 13 Mar 2026 and 26 May 2026; fetched 2026-10-28; third-party list).

| Game | Visits or players as shown | Progression |
|---|---|---|
| Tower Defense Simulator | 1.5 billion+ visits | No gacha; unlocks through play and coins; two upgrade paths per tower (offence or support) |
| Toilet Tower Defense | not given; called most played TD in 2024 to 2025 | Gacha rarity tiers Common to Mythical; units evolve or upgrade; limited event units |
| All Star Tower Defense X | not given (original credited 1.5B+) | Summon Gate gacha; units evolve through tiers; limited event units; cap of 5 per tower type |
| Anime Defenders | not shown | Gacha; skill tree with branching paths to five final builds; element matchups, traits |
| Tower Defense X | not shown | No gacha; progression through tower upgrade paths |
| Universal Tower Defense | not shown | No gacha; no progression described |
| Anime Dimensions Simulator | not shown | Gacha; idle rewards; no evolve or merge described |

The article does not mention merge or fuse for any game.

## Visit figures (trackers disagree)
- All Star Tower Defense: a tracker sidebar shows 7,953.7M total visits, while its article text says "over 7.67 billion"; peak players 53K (March 2026). [RoWatcher, ASTD](https://rowatcher.com/games/1720936166/og-all-star-tower-defense) (fetched 2026-10-28). A search summary of another site gives "1.5B+" for the same original title, so the figures conflict.
- Toilet Tower Defense: about 6.1 billion visits (6,075,928,097 on one tracker). [Bloxodes, Toilet Tower Defense](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/episode-79-part-2-toilet-tower-defense-4778845442) (search summary only, undated). The game is labelled "Legacy" on another tracker.
- Toilet Verse Tower Defense: 88.8M visits ([RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/game/5371127625/Toilet-Verse-Tower-Defense), search summary).

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not sourced)
1. Evolve through tiers is the common loop in the two biggest gacha titles; our merge-to-tier loop is in the same family. Confirming how they gate it needs a play session (not done).
2. Unit caps per type (5 per type in ASTD X) is a design lever to keep rosters varied (T185).
3. Two upgrade paths per tower (TDS) is the simplest identity fork, matching Bloons ([[Bloons TD6 upgrades and paths]]) and T196.
4. Limited-time event units drive return visits but conflict with our no-dark-pattern plan (T189, T240); prefer permanent unlock events.
