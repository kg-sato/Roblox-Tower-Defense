# Arcane (Netflix): character art direction and lighting vocabulary (T005)

Question: what art-direction and lighting rules does Arcane use, and what should our mystic-Victorian look take from it?

## Facts (sourced)
- Season 1 aired 6 to 20 Nov 2021; season 2 aired 9 to 23 Nov 2024. Made by French studio Fortiche for Riot Games, distributed by Netflix. Reviewers describe a 2D and 3D blend; one says frames resemble hand-painted concept art. [Wikipedia, Arcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcane_(TV_series)) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 280,511 characters read).
- Rough, scratchy, hand-painted textures are credited with a timeless look versus 3D styles that date; imperfections make the world feel authentic. 3D gives camera movement and depth; 2D gives painterly "soul". [AWN, "Unveiling Arcane" (Charrue and Wanneroy)](https://awn.com/animationworld/unveiling-arcane-conversation-pascal-charrue-and-alexis-wanneroy) (21 Jan 2025; fetched 2026-10-28).
- Characters were stripped of game-style exaggeration and built around motivation, history and vulnerability; clothing was designed to tell story. Same source.
- Piltover got a shape language of geometry, symmetry and mechanical beauty, then a weathered Art Deco "City of Progress" look. Zaun was made as its dark mirror: Victorian and old industrial architecture with asymmetrical, handcrafted, upcycled ornament. [80.lv, Arnaud-Loris Baudry interview](https://80.lv/articles/riot-games-on-designing-arcane-s-piltover-zaun/) (19 Sep 2023; fetched 2026-10-28).
- A shared library of set pieces, props and storefronts kept streets distinct yet consistent; about a dozen landmarks guide the viewer. Same source.
- Lighting and colour: the two fetched interviews do not discuss them. Palette claims (Piltover bright whites, blues, golds; Zaun neon greens, purples, pinks with smoke) come only from search summaries of lower-quality pages ([Enlo News](https://enloenews.org/7679/arts-entertainment/the-beauty-of-arcane/)); treat as unverified. The Riot interview summary (AWN, "Riot Games and Fortiche push every possible boundary", not read) says palette and lighting follow the narrative arcs.

## Takeaways for us (recommendations, not facts)
1. Two paired looks that mirror each other (our Lantern Keepers vs Hollow Court, or lamplit streets vs the cosmic sky) with one shape-language sentence each; write it into the tone guide (T057) and character bible (T056).
2. Add painted roughness to textures instead of clean gradients: hand-painted base and roughness maps (T091, T123).
3. Build a prop and set-piece library per stage set, plus landmarks that guide the eye (T162).
4. Lighting vocabulary must be derived from a read source; this note does not supply one. Source the book "The Art and Making of Arcane" or Fortiche talks before copying any palette rule.
