# Hollow Knight boss design (T032)

Question: does Hollow Knight give each boss one mechanic, how do arenas and telegraphs work, and what should our five bosses take?

**Finding:** no source supports a strict "one mechanic per boss" rule. Search snippets suggest several bosses (Hornet, the Radiance) use more than one. The note records what is sourced and keeps the rest as labelled opinion.

## Facts (sourced)
- Bosses named on the Wikipedia page: Hornet (tests the Knight's combat skill), the Hollow Knight (final boss of the base game), the Radiance (source of the Infection; beatable with the Void Heart charm), Grimm (Grimm Troupe expansion, Nightmare King form), the Pantheons of Hallownest in Godhome (five boss rushes beaten in a row without dying), Absolute Radiance. Defeating some bosses grants new abilities; bosses gate progress in many areas. Reviews: Eurogamer called it "ruthlessly tough, even occasionally unfair"; Destructoid said it never found a boss unfair; reviewers described combat as simple but nuanced. [Wikipedia, Hollow Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_Knight) (fetched 2026-10-28). The page has no per-boss design notes.
- The developers wanted players to feel any hit could have been avoided up to the final moment (a fairness goal), from the Knight's instant horizontal movement, high jump and strong mid-air control. Discovered encounters were meant to feel like rewards for exploration. The article does not discuss boss design, arena design or telegraphs. [Game Informer, "The Making of Hollow Knight"](https://gameinformer.com/2018/10/16/the-making-of-hollow-knight) (15 Oct 2018; fetched 2026-10-28).
- The Grimm fight is described by a blog as having a clear animation per attack, steady speed and a dodge window ([Choost Games](https://choostgames.com/blog/what-makes-a-good-boss-fight/), 21 Apr 2026; see [[Boss telegraphs]]).
- Snippets from guide and listicle pages (low authority, not read in full): the Mantis Lords are an early teaching fight for pattern recognition; Hornet has a parry and suspended spike balls; the Radiance has timed platforms and phases; one fan piece says the game lets you learn one mechanic before it combines two. Examples: [Pocket Tactics, bosses](https://www.pockettactics.com/hollow-knight/bosses), [Nintendo Life, boss list](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/hollow-knight-boss-locations-and-moves-list).

## Not found
- Arena shape facts (sizes, layout) and timings: none read. No developer statement on boss design rules.

## Recommendations for our five bosses (not sourced rules)
1. Give each boss one defining mechanic as the thing to learn (Unbound: fog that hides lanes; Automaton: slowed board; Echo: pushes familiars; Regent: summons mourners; Clockmaker: undoes a merge), then add a second mechanic only in a later phase, as the snippets say the game does. This matches T143 to T147.
2. Make the arena serve the mechanic (T172 to T176): wide and open for fog, a ring for gears, open sky for the choir, pews as clear space, a stopped Foundry for the final.
3. Keep each attack's windup at constant speed with a clear tell and a recovery window (T187 as data, T138 telegraph).
4. Teach in an early fight first: the stage 5 boss should be the "Mantis Lords" of our game, readable and fair.
5. Phase transitions with a clear visual and sound change (T148, T149).
