# Cinematic Cutscenes (research + first-person ultimate)

The user: "research how to make cutscenes with high quality and shaders / animations"; then "Ultimate first-person". Related: [[Planning/Immersion - Every Action a Cutscene]], [[Crafted Sheet Techniques]].

## How good Roblox cutscenes are made (sources)
- **Author camera relative to a reference part.** Moon Animator exports one CFrame per frame (60 fps); playback in `RenderStepped` advances `FrameTime += dt * 60` and sets `Camera.CFrame = HumanoidRootPart.CFrame * frame` so the same shot works anywhere ([DevForum: Camera Animations for cutscenes and gameplay](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/camera-animations-for-cutscenes-and-gameplay/1181691)). Effects and subtitles are not exported; they are coded (same thread).
- **Smooth paths:** Bézier control points sampled each frame ([CutsceneService](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/cutsceneservice-smooth-cutscenes-using-b%C3%A9zier-curves/718571), [kitsblox guide](https://kitsblox.com/blog/how-to-make-camera-cutscenes-roblox)). Long lens (FOV 20 to 30) for a cinematic look (blog-grade).
- **First-person cutscenes from Blender:** camera at the head, FOV 70, path + empty with Follow Path, bake, a separate bobbing action, Noise modifier on rotation (higher scale, small strength) for handheld feel, export at 60 fps ([DevForum: first-person cutscene with Blender and OAE](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-create-a-first-person-cutscene-using-blender-and-my-oae/2985958)).
- **Anime polish:** impact frame = sharp light/dark contrast about 0.09 s with a 0.02 s fade; radial speed lines from the screen centre; decaying camera shake ([vfx-effects skill guide](https://playbooks.com/skills/taozhuo/game-dev-skills/vfx-effects), [Speedlines FX](https://superhivemarket.com/products/speedlines-fx)); timing is tuned around the impact frames ([Blender Artists breakdown](https://blenderartists.org/t/a-girl-with-super-power-alys-ultimate/1600673)).
- **"Shaders" in Roblox** = Lighting post-processing (Bloom, ColorCorrection, DepthOfField, SunRays, Blur), Highlight, Neon, Beams and particles with LightEmission and Brightness above 1; no custom shader code exists on the platform.
- Not found: an authoritative GDC talk on whip pans or impact timing (GDC Vault pages behind a wall).

### Regression pass (real play, 8 familiars, stages 4 to 10)

Boss key light raised (the boss still read dark on stage 10).

### Cutscenes setting (2026-10-28)
