# Enemy barks (T185)
Three short lines per enemy family, text only, for later recording (no audio exists). Voice from [[Tone Guide]]: calm, wry, dark by implication, no gore, never at the player's expense. Lines are 2 to 8 words so a bark fits in one breath; where the enemy has no voice, the "sound" is described instead. Each family has an idle (while walking), a hit and a leak line (reaches the lantern). Draft for the user to approve; none recorded.

| Family | Idle | Hit | Leak (reaches the lantern) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hollow Parishioner | "Procession... procession..." | "Ah. That was a hand." | "We forgot the way out." |
| Fog Wisp | a thin breath, no words | "Cold. Colder." | "It is only a lamp." |
| Brass Sentinel | "Rounds. Rounds. Rounds." | "Obstruction noted." | "Order received. Order obeyed." |
| Clockwork Crow | a ratchet click, then "Tick." | "Spring! Spring!" | "Mine now. Mine now." |
| Lantern Thief | "Only borrowing." | "Careful! Fragile!" | "You never needed it." |
| The Unbound (boss) | "Nothing happened." | "Undone." | "Nothing happened here." |
| Clockmaker's Automaton (boss) | "Winding. Winding." | "Out of tolerance." | "Resume. Resume." |
| Star Choir's Echo (boss) | a held note, no words | "...ound... ound..." (an echo of a hit sound) | "Sing it back." |
| Hollow Court Regent (boss) | the bell, no words | "The bell is cracked." | "Come in. Light a candle." |
| The Clockmaker (boss) | a slow tick | "Wait." | "One more turn." |

Notes: the Unbound's lines are denial ("nothing happened", "undone") to match the act 1 theme in [[The Clockmaker]]; the Clockmaker's "Wait" and "One more turn" echo the two ending captions. Sound cues described in [[Design/Enemy Bible]] are separate from barks.
