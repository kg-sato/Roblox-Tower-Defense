## The Lantern Keepers
- **Goal:** keep the street lit and the dusk unfinished. They fear a night without lamps more than a city without time.
- **How:** every lamp has a keeper; the youngest keeper (the player) is handed a brass lantern with an empty glass. Keepers shelter familiars in lanterns and walk the rounds.
- **Allies:** the Guild, who repair their lamps. **Enemies:** the Hollow Court, who put lamps out. They distrust the Star Choir, who say the lamps are a cage.
- **Familiars:** Ember, Ward (lantern-glass lighthouse spirit), Moth-Lantern.
- **Flaw:** they would rather keep a bad hour forever than risk a new one.

## The Clockwork Guild
- **Goal:** restart the Great Clock to the second. They keep the records of every hour the city has lost.
- **How:** brass automata, gear-works, patient craft; they trade repairs for access.
- **Allies:** the Keepers. **Enemies:** the Hollow Court (forgetting is rust) and the Star Choir (they want to smash the mechanism). Their machines left running without winding turn hostile: the Brass Sentinels met as enemies.
- **Familiars:** Sentinel, Clockwork Owl (memory keeper), Brass Hound.
- **Flaw:** they cannot admit the Clockmaker, their founder, may not have wanted the clock restarted (see the antagonist task T059).

## The Hollow Court
- **Goal:** let the fog finish. A city that forgets cannot grieve, and the Court believes that is mercy.
- **How:** masked mourners, frost, silence; the Hollow Parishioners met as enemies are their followers.
- **Allies:** none by choice. **Enemies:** the Keepers (they relight what the Court has laid to rest) and the Guild.
- **Familiars:** Frostling, the mourner who gave up its face, Gargoyle (the watcher of the cathedral, bound to the Court's silence).
- **Flaw:** they cannot stop mourning, so they cannot end.

## The Star Choir
- **Goal:** end the clock so the sky can open. They believe the city is a held note and want to sing it out.
- **How:** song, mirrors, constellations; cosmic familiars appear as the stages go on.
- **Allies:** none; they are patient. **Enemies:** the Guild, whose clock holds the note, and, in the end, the Keepers, who will not let the lamps go.
- **Familiars:** Star Drake (last of the Choir), the mirror-cat.
- **Flaw:** the opened sky may not be kind; the Choir does not say what is out there.

## Unaligned
- **Grey Veil Wraith (the unbound):** a boss-tier familiar that belongs to no faction; the fog's own shape, with the Grey Veil as its home.

## Where factions show up in play (proposals)
- Familiar cards carry the faction glyph and colour (T096); the collection can filter by faction (T058).
- Stage sets lean toward a faction: Lampwick Lane (Keepers), Harbour of Brass (Guild), Silent Cathedral (Court), Clockwork Foundry (Guild), Above the Grey Veil (Choir and the unbound); the cosmic turn (T054) is the Choir's doing.
- Adjacent same-faction familiars give a combo bonus (T212).
