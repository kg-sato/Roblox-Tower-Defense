# Stage set lore: history, residents, what went wrong (T055)

Status: draft written overnight 2026-10-28. The five sets and stage ranges come from the project (StageSets data and the task list); everything else is a proposal. It extends [[World Bible]], [[Factions]] and [[The Cosmic Turn]]. Each set lists three landmark props that tell the story without text (see [[Dishonored and Prey environmental lore]]) and the visual notes from the research folder.

## 1. Lampwick Lane (stages 1 to 5): gaslit market street at dusk
- **History:** the oldest street, first lit by hand on the night the Guild laid the gas pipes. Every shop sign still has a lamp bracket.
- **Residents:** the Lantern Keepers' street watch, the Clockmaker's apprentice (a friendly voice, T069) and lamp-sellers (T070); Hollow Parishioners drift in from the fog edges.
- **What went wrong:** the dusk never finishes. The last lamp lit on the night the clock stopped is still the only one that burns on its own.
- **Landmarks:** a lamp row with one dark bracket; a market stall frozen mid-sale; a street clock with its glass cracked and no hands.
- **Faction lean:** Keepers. **Hue:** warm orange and gold on wet cobbles.
- **Boss at stage 5:** the Unbound, a faceless figure of fog (T143).

## 2. Harbour of Brass (stages 6 to 10): quays, cranes, a steamship in the fog
- **History:** the port that fed the city's brass; the Guild's cranes still lift cargo that nobody ordered.
- **Residents:** dockworkers who forgot which shift they were on, Clockwork Crows nesting on the cranes, the Lantern Thief (T137) working the lamps along the quay.
- **What went wrong:** the tide stopped with the clock; ships sit at the same angle on still water, and the verdigris is spreading from the waterline up.
- **Landmarks:** a steamship hull in the top band of the frame (T104); a crane holding a crate over the same spot; bollards with chains that no longer move.
- **Faction lean:** Guild. **Hue:** cool blue-grey fog, verdigris green on brass (see [[Brass verdigris and tarnish references]]).
- **Boss at stage 10:** the Clockmaker's Automaton, a gear-engine that slows the board (T144).

## 3. Silent Cathedral (stages 11 to 15): moonlit nave, covered statues
- **History:** built to hold the city's grief, a place of one bell and many candles. The statues were covered the night after the clock stopped, and nobody remembers who ordered it.
- **Residents:** the Hollow Court's mourners and Frostlings; the Gargoyle on the west front; the Silent Cathedral's candles burn without wax.
- **What went wrong:** the Court taught the congregation to forget, and the forgotten dead are standing in the pews.
- **Landmarks:** stained-glass windows visible at the default camera (T105, see [[Stained glass patterns and distance reading]]); a covered statue nearest the board; a bell with the rope cut.
- **Faction lean:** Hollow Court. **Hue:** moonlit blue-silver with red and blue glass accents.
- **Boss at stage 15:** the Star Choir's Echo (T145), the first sign of the sky.

## 4. Clockwork Foundry (stages 16 to 20): rails, furnaces, gears, sparks
- **History:** where the Guild forged the Great Clock's parts. Furnaces were banked, not put out, when the clock stopped.
- **Residents:** Brass Sentinels walking old patrol routes, Guild engineers who keep winding things that have no use, sparks that act like fireflies.
- **What went wrong:** the machines were left running without winding; they obey the last order they received and treat everything else as an intruder. A crack of white light shows in the roof by stage 20.
- **Landmarks:** a gear section that moves a lane block every 10 seconds (T170); a furnace door still glowing; a rail cart parked at a switch.
- **Faction lean:** Guild. **Hue:** ash brown, ember orange, brass; sulfur-dark tarnish.
- **Boss at stage 20:** the Hollow Court Regent (T146), who has taken the Foundry's bells.

## 5. Above the Grey Veil (stages 21 to 25): the lifted fog, the open sky
- **History:** the roof of the city, a ring of stair and bridge above the fog where the Great Clock's face is mounted. No one lives here by choice.
- **Residents:** the Star Choir, the unaligned Grey Veil Wraith, eyes that watch from the dark, mirror-cats.
- **What went wrong:** nothing; this is where it ends. The sky the fog was hiding is open, and the clock's last hour is waiting.
- **Landmarks:** the clock face seen from behind; a staircase that climbs and comes back (T171); rune circles on the ground.
- **Faction lean:** Choir. **Hue:** deep blue and white on black, lamp orange only at the player's lantern.
- **Final boss at stage 25:** the Clockmaker (T147).
