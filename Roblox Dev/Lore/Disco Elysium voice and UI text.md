# Disco Elysium: voice and UI text (T044)

Question: how does Disco Elysium use inner voices and interface text, and what can we take for narration, captions and tooltips?

**Gap against the task:** only Wikipedia was readable. The gameshub and PC Gamer articles failed (403, truncated), so skill voice examples and UI text styling beyond two colours are not covered.

## Facts (sourced)
- Released on Windows 15 Oct 2019; The Final Cut (full voice acting for nearly 100 characters, including narration and skill voices) followed on 30 Mar 2021 on PS4, PS5 and Stadia, and 12 Oct 2021 on Switch and Xbox. Led by Estonian novelist Robert Kurvitz with lead writer Helen Hindpere and eight dialogue writers; Hindpere said the script totals over 1.2 million words. [Wikipedia, Disco Elysium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco_Elysium) (fetched 2026-10-28; first 100,000 of 154,992 characters).
- 24 skills grouped under four attributes (Intellect, Psyche, Physique, Motorics); each skill is an internal voice speaking to the player with its own personality, sometimes flawed. Checks roll two six-sided dice; high skills have drawbacks. Same source.
- Thought Cabinet: thoughts unlocked in conversation are "internalised" over in-game time for benefits, often with drawbacks; slots grow with level; a skill point can forget a thought. Same source.
- Interface: conversation text appears in white and player choices in orange, over the current scene; the art is painterly (Aleksander Rostov). Won Best Narrative at The Game Awards 2019. Same source.
- From search snippets (unread): higher skills speak more often and more forcefully ([PC Gamer, Disco Elysium puts a full party of NPC companions in your head](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/disco-elysium-puts-a-full-party-of-npc-companions-in-your-head), only the headline loaded); Hindpere said the biggest puzzle is the player's own self ([GameBanshee interview](https://gamebanshee.com/39r8d), snippet); Kurvitz discusses the Thought Cabinet in a GameSpot Audio Logs interview ([RPGCodex summary](https://rpgcodex.net/article.php?id=11309), snippet).

## Not found

## Recommendations (not sourced)
1. Make the Lantern's voices a small cast: three to five narration voices (the Lantern, the Clockmaker's Apprentice, the fog) each with a consistent tone and colour, used for captions and tooltips (T057, T069).
2. Let a voice speak more when it matters: tie caption frequency to a stat or event, such as bond level, so voices are earned (T207).
3. Keep UI body text plain white on dark and choices in one accent colour, as in the dialogue UI above, and check contrast (accessibility note).
4. A "thought" style slow-burn unlock (a lore line that internalises over a number of stages) is a good model for lore fragments (T071).
5. Keep lines short for a phone: the volume of Disco Elysium's script (1.2 million words) is not a model for us; one to two lines per beat.
