## Card
- **Name and epithet:** Cantor Vesper, who keeps the Choir's last note.
- **Faction and element:** Star Choir; starlight and breath. **Rarity:** Mythic. **Role (one verb):** Hymn (touching familiars deal 20% more damage). **Home set:** Above the Grey Veil.
- **Signature hue:** a silver-blue greatcoat `#6E8DA8` with gold trim `#C9A24B`, short silver hair, a small gold star crown and a slender baton of light.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#AABEFF`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; the body colours above are unchanged.
- **Lore line (20 words):** Vesper sang the last note of the Choir into the fog so the city would remember it was a song.
- **Gesture:** raises the baton, holds the note, and waits for the others to join.
- **Flaw:** Hymn only lifts the familiars standing next to her, so she is wasted on an empty tile.
- **Voice (3 words):** calm, exact, tireless. Lines: summon "Take your breath. Now, together." Attack "Hold the note." Victory "The song goes on without us."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 110 words)

Every set in the city has a bell, and the Silent Cathedral's has no rope. Vesper is why the city still knows what the bell was for. She stood in the nave on the night the clock stopped and kept singing, one note, for as long as there was anyone to hear it, and the note became the Star Choir. She wears a greatcoat the colour of a late sky and a small crown she says is only a pin. Familiars who stand beside her fight a little better and do not know why. She does: it is the note, and it is for them. She asks only that they keep the time, and that when the fog comes in, someone hums.

## What the model shows
A tall figure in a long A-line greatcoat with a high stiff collar, star-shaped buttons, a short silver cut and a small crown of stars; the right hand holds a thin baton of light.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- **A tall, narrow coat with a collar flare and a thin vertical baton:** unlike the hooded or caped humans, her outline is a long A-line with one thin line beside it.
- The baton is the readable part at 48 px; the crown is too small and is carried by the model's hair.
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second baton in the free hand (the Choir answers in two voices now); tier 3 adds a ring of seven small stars round the crown (the whole Choir stands behind her). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).
