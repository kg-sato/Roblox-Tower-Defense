## Card
- **Name and epithet:** Constable Wren, who blows the hour the clock forgot.
- **Faction and element:** Lantern Keepers; brass and breath. **Rarity:** Epic. **Role (one verb):** Whistle (every fourth shot is a whistle blast that hits nearby enemies for 60% damage). **Home set:** Lampwick Lane.
- **Signature hue:** a dark blue watch coat `#2F3E57` with brass buttons `#B8863B`, a round helmet and a brass whistle on a chain.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#E0A458`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; UNVERIFIED for colour-blind safety, not yet tested.
- **Lore line (20 words):** Wren walks the lane at the hour no clock rings, and blows once so the street knows it is still there.
- **Gesture:** lifts the whistle, waits a beat, and blows.
- **Flaw:** the blast only reaches enemies bunched near the target, so a thin line barely feels it.
- **Voice (3 words):** dutiful, dry, unshakeable. Lines: summon "Evening, all. Keep moving." Attack "Move along." Victory "Lights on. Another night kept."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 100 words)

Before the Keepers had lanterns they had constables, and Wren is the last of them. She was sworn to a beat that has since been paved over by the fog, and walks it anyway. The truncheon is for show; the whistle is the weapon, one clean note the fog cannot swallow. Familiars who stand near her stop looking over their shoulders. She does not smile much. She does keep a list of every lamp she has seen lit, and reads it aloud when nobody is listening. On the nights the fog is thick she walks the beat twice, once for the street and once for anyone who has forgotten there was a street. Children say her whistle sounds warmer on the second pass, and nobody has ever dared to ask her why.

## What the model shows
A broad-shouldered woman in a long dark watch coat with brass buttons, a round helmet and a whistle on a chain at her collar, a short truncheon at her belt.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- A wide helmet and a squared coat, with the whistle chain as the readable detail; unlike the tall narrow humans her outline is a sturdy rectangle.
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second whistle on the chain (the beat is doubled now); tier 3 adds a ring of small lit lanterns floating round her shoulders (every lamp on her list answers). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).
