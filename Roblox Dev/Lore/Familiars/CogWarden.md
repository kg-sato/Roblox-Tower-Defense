## Card
- **Name and epithet:** The Cog-Warden, who holds the Foundry door shut.
- **Faction and element:** Clockwork Guild; gears and iron. **Rarity:** Legendary. **Role (one verb):** Lockstep (crits stun the target, and every sixth shot strikes twice). **Home set:** Clockwork Foundry.
- **Signature hue:** a heavy black iron half-plate `#2B2B30` with brass gearwork `#B8863B`, a visored helm and a great key at the belt.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#E6B450`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; UNVERIFIED for colour-blind safety, not yet tested.
- **Lore line (20 words):** The Warden has not opened the Foundry in a hundred years, and no one has found out what is inside by asking.
- **Gesture:** plants the key-staff into the ground and the floor answers.
- **Flaw:** Lockstep only rewards patience; a familiar that rarely crits is just a heavy wall.
- **Voice (3 words):** slow, courteous, immovable. Lines: summon "The door stays shut. Stand behind me." Attack "Not yet." Victory "It was worth the wait."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 100 words)

Nobody remembers who gave the Warden the key, and the Warden does not say. The armour is older than the Guild and was never made to be taken off. He moves slowly because everything he does is permanent. Where the Warden stands, the Foundry's gears tick once for every blow, and when one lands wrong the enemy does not get back up for a moment. He is unfailingly polite. He will tell you the door is not for you, and then he will tell you to mind your step. On quiet nights the gears in the walls tick in time with his breathing, and the street lamps nearby burn a little steadier. The Guild's engineers call this a coincidence. He has never corrected them, because it would be impolite to be right.

## What the model shows
A very large armoured figure in black iron plate with brass gears at the joints, a visored helm, a long key-shaped staff in both hands, a heavy cloak of chain.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- The broadest outline in the roster: a wide pauldron line and a tall key-staff, readable even when small.
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second great key on the other hip (two doors held now); tier 3 adds a halo of slow turning gears behind the helm (the Foundry turns with him). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).
