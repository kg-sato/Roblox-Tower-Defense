## Card
- **Name and epithet:** Madam Quill, the Ink Seer who writes tomorrow's footnotes.
- **Faction and element:** Star Choir; ink and starlight. **Rarity:** Epic. **Role (one verb):** Annotate (each hit marks the enemy and hushes its special move). **Home set:** Above the Grey Veil.
- **Signature hue:** a deep violet robe `#3B2F5C` with silver script embroidered along the hem `#BFC5D8`, ink-stained fingers and a tall pen.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#9A8CFF`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; UNVERIFIED for colour-blind safety, not yet tested.
- **Lore line (20 words):** Quill writes what will happen in the margin of what did, and the fog has learned to read her handwriting.
- **Gesture:** touches the pen to the air and a word hangs there.
- **Flaw:** she marks everything, so a lone strong enemy is hushed but never slowed.
- **Voice (3 words):** precise, amused, faintly ominous. Lines: summon "Ah. A new line in the ledger." Attack "Noted." Victory "As written."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 100 words)

Madam Quill keeps a book that is a little larger on the inside, and she has never once opened it to the first page. She writes in the margins: small corrections to the night, a word here that makes a gear stick, a note there that makes a mourner forget its cue. Enemies she has annotated move as if remembering something unpleasant. She is not cruel. She is thorough, and she believes the city is a draft that deserves a good edit. She never explains an annotation. If you ask, she smiles and turns the page so you can see that it has already been written, in your own handwriting, a few minutes before you asked. The fog, which cannot read, has still learned to be afraid of ink.

## What the model shows
A tall thin woman in a long robe with script along the hem, a wide-brimmed hat tilted forward, a very tall pen held like a staff, a heavy book under one arm.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- A hat brim and a very tall pen: a vertical line with a wide cap, distinct from Cantor Vesper's baton because the pen sits in a tilted hat-and-book mass.
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second floating pen that writes on its own (the margins fill faster); tier 3 adds the book opens and pages of light circle her (the whole draft is visible now). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).
