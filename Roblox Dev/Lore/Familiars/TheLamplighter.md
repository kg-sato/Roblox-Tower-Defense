## Card
- **Name and epithet:** The Lamplighter, who lit the first lamp.
- **Faction and element:** Lantern Keepers; starlight and dusk. **Rarity:** Celestial. **Role (one verb):** Kindle (every familiar on the board deals 10% more damage). **Home set:** Lampwick Lane.
- **Signature hue:** a long dark coat patterned with small stars `#2A2420`, a brown hood and scarf `#7A5A3C`, worn boots, a tall pole topped by a glowing star lantern `#FFD678`.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#FFD678`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; the body colours above are unchanged.
- **Lore line (20 words):** Before there was a Lane there was one lamp and one person with the patience to keep it lit.
- **Gesture:** lifts the pole an inch, and every lamp along the street leans toward it.
- **Flaw:** Kindle is a small bonus for everyone, so a lone strong familiar gains more from a stronger neighbour.
- **Voice (3 words):** patient, quiet, kind. Lines: summon "Light enough for all of you." Attack "Here. Look." Victory "Another lamp lit. Another."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 110 words)

Lampwick Lane has seven lamps and a story that the first of them lit itself. The Lamplighter knows better and does not argue. They walked the unbuilt street at dusk with a pole and a flame from somewhere further than the Lane, and the Keepers grew around that flame. They wear a coat sewn with small stars because the first night was cold and the stars were the only company. Nobody has seen their whole face under the hood, only a weathered smile. When they stand on the board, every familiar fights in a slightly better light, and none of them can say who turned it up. The Keepers leave one seat at their table empty and say it is for the lamp, and nobody argues, because nobody is sure.

## What the model shows
A tall hooded figure in a long dark coat with a star pattern, a scarf, worn boots, a belted sash and a long pole with a glowing star-shaped lantern at its top held in the left hand.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- **A hooded figure with a very tall pole and lantern:** the pole makes the highest, thinnest vertical in the cast, and the lantern is the one warm point.
- The pole is thin, so the silhouette checks should be read with the lantern at the top.
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second small lantern hung from the belt (one for the street, one for the walker); tier 3 adds a halo of tiny stars above the hood (the first night's company has come back). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).
