# Familiar 16: Veil Walker (human, Celestial)

Status: written 2026-10-29 at the user's request ("higher rarities should have more human characters"). Follows [[Character Bible Rules]] and [[Cast List - 12 Familiars]]. This is one of four human familiars for the new Mythic and Celestial rarities. Concept images by ComfyUI (FLUX schnell), shape by Hunyuan3D-2 mini-Turbo, textures projected from the front and back concepts, rigged with the biped template, recoloured in the gold-ink palette ([[Gold Ink Recolour]]). Files: `Roblox Dev/Assets/Characters/Concept/VeilWalker_*.png`, `Roblox Dev/Assets/3D/Familiars/rigged/VeilWalker_rigged.glb`.

## Card
- **Name and epithet:** Veil Walker, who walks above the fog.
- **Faction and element:** Unaligned; fog and starlight. **Rarity:** Celestial. **Role (one verb):** Veil (every familiar on the board gains 12% critical-hit chance). **Home set:** Above the Grey Veil.
- **Signature hue:** a hooded cape of thin bronze veils `#9A7340` over a plain tunic, a gold mirror-like mask `#C9A24B` pushed back, bare feet and bracelets of small stars.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#BEE6FF`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; the body colours above are unchanged.
- **Lore line (20 words):** The Walker crosses the Grey Veil on foot, and every step leaves the fog a little less sure of itself.
- **Gesture:** pushes the mask up, looks at the enemy once, and the enemy looks away.
- **Flaw:** Veil raises everyone's critical chance, so it does nothing for a team that cannot land hits.
- **Voice (3 words):** light, distant, amused. Lines: summon "Mind the edge. It is a long way down." Attack "There. See it?" Victory "The fog will remember this."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 110 words)

Nobody knows who the Veil Walker was before the fog, and the Walker does not seem to mind. They cross the roofs of the city above the Grey Veil on bare feet, in a cape so thin it hangs on the wind, with a mask that shows a face a little different to everyone who looks. Familiars who stand near the Walker notice the gaps in their enemies sooner, and they hit them. The Walker says it is only a matter of looking from above. The Star Choir says it is a gift. The fog says nothing, and moves aside. If you ask the Walker where they are going, the answer is always the same: up, and a little to the left of where you are looking.

## What the model shows
A slender figure in a hooded cape of thin veils that hang to the knees over a plain tunic, a gold mask on the face, bare feet and star bracelets.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- **A slim column with a long veil cape and no feet shadow:** it reads as a thin shape with a drape on both sides, between the Mourner's veil and the Wraith's trail but with visible legs.
- The side and back colours are the front's per-row median (the back concept showed bare skin, so it was not used).
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second veil layer in a paler colour (more of the sky follows them); tier 3 adds a hovering ring of three small stars at the shoulder (the sky walks with them). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).

## Known flaws
Generated shape and projected texture, not hand-modelled: soft hands and faces, the side and back colours are inferred, no Idle clip yet, stats are placeholders (see [[Combat Systems Implemented]]).
