Status: written 2026-10-29 at the user's request ("higher rarities should have more human characters"). Follows [[Character Bible Rules]] and [[Cast List - 12 Familiars]]. ## Card
- **Name and epithet:** Dame Orrery, who winds what others abandon.
- **Faction and element:** Clockwork Guild; brass and oil. **Rarity:** Mythic. **Role (one verb):** Rewind (every tenth shot resets the cooldown of touching familiars). **Home set:** Clockwork Foundry.
- **Signature hue:** a dark work coat `#2A2420` under a leather apron `#6B4A2E`, brass mechanical forearms `#C9A24B`, grey hair and round goggles.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#E2AA50`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; the body colours above are unchanged.
- **Lore line (20 words):** Orrery has repaired every clock in the Foundry twice, and the ones that stopped on purpose she leaves alone.
- **Gesture:** taps the wrench on her shoulder twice, then points it at whatever is slow.
- **Flaw:** Rewind only fires on her tenth shot, so a short fight ends before it helps.
- **Voice (3 words):** gruff, warm, impatient. Lines: summon "Right. What is broken?" Attack "Again, faster." Victory "Good. Now oil it."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 110 words)

The Clockwork Guild gave Orrery the title of Dame the year she rebuilt the Foundry's bellows by hand, and she has refused to wear anything but the apron since. Her forearms are brass, built in the same shop that built the Great Clock, and she will not say what the originals did wrong. She keeps a wrench on her shoulder and a list of everything in the city that is slow. Her gift is simple: every few shots, whoever is beside her gets their moment back. She calls it winding. Everyone else calls it a miracle, and she calls that nonsense. Ask her for advice and she will hand you a rag and point at something that needs polishing; it is, you will find, the advice.

## What the model shows
A broad figure with a heavy leather apron over a dark coat, brass forearms and hands, round goggles pushed up on grey hair and a large wrench resting on the shoulder.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- **A wide, solid figure with one diagonal wrench:** the widest human of the cast; the wrench makes a unique diagonal at the shoulder.
- Floating chips of the wrench from the first shape were cut out (13 loose parts).
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second wrench on the belt (she keeps a spare for whoever asks); tier 3 adds a brass gear brooch pinned to the apron (the Guild finally pinned its mark on her). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).

## Known flaws
Generated shape and projected texture, not hand-modelled: soft hands and faces, the side and back colours are inferred, no Idle clip yet, stats are placeholders (see [[Combat Systems Implemented]]).
