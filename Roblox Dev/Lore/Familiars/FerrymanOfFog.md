## Card
- **Name and epithet:** The Ferryman of Fog, who carries what the fog forgets.
- **Faction and element:** Hollow Court; fog and cold water. **Rarity:** Legendary. **Role (one verb):** Crossing (each hit chills and marks the enemy, and he extends the reach of familiars beside him by 15%). **Home set:** Harbour of Brass.
- **Signature hue:** a long hooded grey-green coat `#3C4A48` dripping pale water `#8FD3D0`, a tall punt pole and a hanging lantern with cold light.
- **Identity colour (T088, colour-blind tested):** `#8FD3D0`, used for the card frame, name plate and glow; UNVERIFIED for colour-blind safety, not yet tested.
- **Lore line (20 words):** The Ferryman will take anything across, including the hour you wanted to keep, and he is never late.
- **Gesture:** sets the pole and pushes off from nothing.
- **Flaw:** he is a patient helper but a slow hitter; against a fast rush he is a ferry that leaves late.
- **Voice (3 words):** quiet, kind, tired. Lines: summon "Step in. Mind the water." Attack "Cold, isn't it." Victory "Everyone gets across."

## Longer bio (collection card, about 100 words)

The Court says mercy is letting the fog finish. The Ferryman has never quite agreed. He poles a flat black boat along the harbour wall at the hour the water goes still, and he will carry whatever you give him to the other shore without asking what it is. Enemies he touches slow, as if remembering the cold. Familiars beside him seem to see a little further. He does not sing and he does not speak much, and when he does it is to say the water is higher than it looks. The fare is whatever you can spare: a coin, a name, a bad memory. He keeps none of it. He says the other shore is a long way off, and that everything he is given gets there a little lighter than it left.

## What the model shows
A tall cloaked figure in a long hooded coat that drips pale water, holding a tall punt pole in one hand and a hanging lantern in the other, a flat-bottomed boat hull trailing like a train.

## Silhouette and design decisions
- Hood, pole and a hanging lantern: a tall thin vertical with a small light, different from Lamplighter's pole because the hood is closed and the lantern hangs below it.
- Merge tiers (T100, final): tier 2 adds a second lantern, lower, on the pole's tail (two lights across the water); tier 3 adds a low ring of mist and slow ripples round his boots (the harbour is with him). The figure grows to 115 and then 130 percent. Body-attached, so it never clashes with the rarity kit ([[Rarity Silhouettes]]).
