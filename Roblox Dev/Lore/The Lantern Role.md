# The player's Lantern role: backstory and why they summon familiars (T061)

Status: draft written overnight 2026-10-28. The player is "a new Lantern Keeper" in [[World Bible]]; this page adds the oath, the lantern's rules and the reason for summoning. All specifics are proposals. It supports [[Tutorial Story Beats]], [[Factions]] and [[The Clockmaker]], and the trainer props and outfit tasks (T112, T113). The player is never named or gendered; the text addresses "you".

## Who you are
You are the youngest Lantern Keeper on Lampwick Lane. The older Keepers tend fixed street lamps and cannot leave their posts; their light is strong but it cannot move. The Keepers kept one brass lantern in reserve, with a glass that has never held a flame, for a Keeper who could walk. That is you. You were chosen because you were nobody's apprentice and the fog had not yet taken anything from you to hold over you (a gentle way of saying the fog has not found your name).

## The lantern's rules
1. **It cannot light itself.** The glass is empty until a kept hour stands beside it. That is why you summon: a familiar is an hour that has agreed to stay near a light.
2. **It never commands.** A familiar chooses the lantern's side. This is why merging is framed as teaching, not forcing (see the lines in [[Tutorial Story Beats]]).
3. **It lights the lane around it, not the lane ahead.** The fog is held back only where familiars stand, so placing them is the whole job.
4. **It remembers what it has lit.** The glass slowly fills as stages are cleared; at the last stage it shows what it has gathered. The skill tree is that picture: the future the lantern shows when it burns bright.

## The oath (short, for a loading line)
"I keep the light. I keep what stays. I let the hour go when it asks." The last clause matters: it is what lets the ending turn on whether to restart the clock (see [[The Clockmaker]]).

## Why you summon familiars
- **In the story:** the fog hides lanes and takes hours from the city; familiars are small kept hours that still remember how to stand. Summoning is asking one to stand with you; rolling is the lantern looking for the hour that will come.
- **In the mechanics:** coins spend on the roll (the lantern's look), the server decides the result, and a duplicate hour joins an existing one at a higher tier (the merge). Nothing here changes the code; it is the reason the code exists.
- **In the tone:** polite and gentle, never owning or commanding anyone (see [[Tone Guide]]).

## Keeper ranks (proposal for the trainer level names)
Trainer levels in the game give gems and luck. A rank title per band gives the number a name: Wick (levels 1 to 9), Flame (10 to 19), Lamp (20 to 29), Beacon (30 and up). Names are short and concrete ([[Naming in whimsical dark fantasy]]). The band thresholds are mine; the trainer progression in Config would need a separate task to display them.

## Look (for T112 and T113)
- A long coat the colour of old lamp-oil, a satchel of spare wicks, a brass lantern with an empty glass at the hip. Four outfit variants (task list) vary the coat colour and the lantern frame.
- The lantern is the brightest object in any scene; the glass holds a small ember-coloured point of light once Ember is summoned.
