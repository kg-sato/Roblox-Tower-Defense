# Lore fragments: 60 collectibles, 12 per chapter (T071)

Status: written overnight 2026-10-28. Each fragment is a found object or a short written scrap, 25 words or fewer, in the voice of [[Tone Guide]]. They plant and echo the clues in [[The Clockmaker]] and the chapter scripts ([[Chapter 1 - Lampwick Lane]] to [[Chapter 5 - Above the Grey Veil]]). The data lives in `Studio_Export/LoreFragments.luau` (ReplicatedStorage.Shared.LoreFragments in Studio) and a headless test in `Tests/LoreFragments.test.luau`. What is NOT built: placing each fragment in a stage, picking one up, and saving finds in the profile (the server decides what a player has found; see the collection schema task T186).

Chapters: 1 Lampwick Lane (stages 1 to 5), 2 Harbour of Brass (6 to 10), 3 Silent Cathedral (11 to 15), 4 Clockwork Foundry (16 to 20), 5 Above the Grey Veil (21 to 25).
