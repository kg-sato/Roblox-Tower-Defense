# Trainer Props (T112)

Three designs each for the Lantern, a satchel and a coat, from [[The Lantern Role]] ("a long coat the colour of old lamp-oil, a satchel of spare wicks, a brass lantern with an empty glass at the hip"). Concept drawings by code, front view: `tools/prop_sheet.py`, sheet `Assets/Characters/Props/_props_sheet.png`. Look UNVERIFIED by the user. T113 (the trainer outfit) uses one of each.

| Prop | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lantern | **Keeper's Lamp**: brass cage, glass cylinder, ring handle, side hook (the main design) | **Fog Lantern**: tall hexagonal frame with shutter louvres and a pocket-watch dial in the base | **Walking Globe**: round wire-caged globe with a small bell, hung from a leather strap |
| Satchel | **Wick Messenger**: leather bag with a flap, brass buckle, spare wicks (unlit) poking out | **Gear Pouch**: pouch with a gear clasp and a pocket-watch chain | **Roll-top Wick Bag**: canvas roll-top with a lantern emblem and a wick bundle strapped on the side |
| Coat | **Lamp-oil Long Coat**: knee length, high collar, double row of brass buttons, belt with ring | **Hooded Fog Cloak**: grey, hooded, brass clasp (the fog-stage outfit) | **Harbour Jacket**: short, epaulettes, two lantern-hook loops at the hem |

Lore rules checked: the lantern glass stays empty ("it cannot light itself": no flame-coloured pixels in any glass); the lantern is the brightest object on the sheet; coats A and C are old lamp-oil (hue 39, dark), B is deliberately grey for the fog stage; spare wicks are drawn unlit.
