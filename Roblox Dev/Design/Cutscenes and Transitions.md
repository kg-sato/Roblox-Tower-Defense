# Cutscenes and transitions added 2026-10-29 (Sonnet, at the user's request: "make more cutscenes if possible. Morph or other transitions are also encouraged")
All three live in `StarterPlayerScripts` and are driven by the server's existing events, so GameClient (the loading-eye chat's) was not touched. Installed in Studio and mirrored in `Studio_Export/`; **seen once in Play** on 2026-10-29 through captures and property polling; not saved in the place file; the match HUD was not on screen (the start menu was), so the placement over the HUD is unchecked.

| Piece | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Set morph | `StageSetClient.luau` | A set change is no longer a snap: a fog-coloured veil rises (0.6 s), the backdrop and music swap behind it, then Lighting and Atmosphere ease to the new set over 2.4 s (ClockTime takes the short way round the clock) while the veil lifts. First set, `cinematics` other than full, and `reducedMotion` still snap. Seen: veil in the Harbour fog colour, then ClockTime 18.60, Brightness 1.20, veil transparency 1.0, CurrentSet HarbourOfBrass. |
| Story captions | `CutsceneDirector.luau` + `Shared.StageCaptions` (generated by `tools/gen_stage_captions.py` from the 40 captions in `Lore/Story/Stage Captions.md`) | Gold bracketed typewriter captions for stage intros (once per stage per run), boss warnings, boss defeated and set clear, one at a time. Seen: `[ THE HARBOUR OF BRASS ]`, `[ IT WEARS NO FACE ]`, typewriter in progress. |
| Boss-defeat cut | `CutsceneDirector.luau` | The world drains of colour for a beat (saturation -0.7), then a warm bloom, then a fall of gold lantern-ash that follows the camera. Seen by polling: saturation -0.70, then 0.15, then 0.06; ash emitting 90 per second. |
| Event captions | same | `[ A CROW PERCHES ]`, `[ A LAMP GOES DARK ]`, `[ THE LIGHT RETURNS ]` on the new enemy events, at most one per 20 s each. Seen: Steal caption. |

Settings respected (from the `Settings` event): `cinematics` off shows no ash or grade (captions stay), short shows captions only, `reducedMotion` shows captions without the typewriter, grade or ash, and `flashes` false drops the brightness flash. Debug hook: fire `ReplicatedStorage.DirectorDebug` with (kind, data).

Not done: a merge morph (a familiar visibly transforming when it tiers up needs GameClient's model table), a boss phase transition (bosses have no phases in the sim yet), a Telegraph vignette (the Brass Sentinel telegraphs every 4 s, so it would be noisy), the title-to-match transition (loading-eye chat), and a Fog Wisp lamp scene. Sounds for the captions and morph are not added.
