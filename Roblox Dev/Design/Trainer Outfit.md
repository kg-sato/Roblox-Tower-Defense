# Trainer Outfit (T113)

The player's Lantern Keeper in four cosmetic variants, one per rank band from [[The Lantern Role]]: **Wick** (levels 1 to 9), **Flame** (10 to 19), **Lamp** (20 to 29), **Beacon** (30 and up). Same body and props in all four (long coat A, wick satchel, Keeper's Lamp from [[Trainer Props]]); only the coat colour and the lantern frame metal change. Concept figure drawn by `tools/trainer_sheet.py`, sheet `Assets/Characters/Trainer/_trainer_sheet.png`. This is a 2D concept, not a Roblox model or avatar outfit yet; look UNVERIFIED by the user.

| Variant | Coat | Lantern frame |
|---|---|---|
| Wick | old lamp-oil `#5C461E` (the lore-literal outfit) | brass `#C9A24B` |
| Flame | ember red-brown `#B44E32`, lighter than Wick so colour-blind players can tell them apart | copper `#BE6C3E` |
| Lamp | verdigris `#26625C` | brass |
| Beacon | midnight blue `#223068` | silver `#C4CADA` |

The player is never named or gendered ([[The Lantern Role]]), so the figure has a cap and two dots, no face detail. The lantern glass is empty in every variant. Rank titles are the lore note's proposal; whether the HUD shows them is an open question there.
Open decisions: how the variants are unlocked (by rank band, or chosen freely); whether the model is built as an R15 avatar outfit (Roblox layered clothing) or a custom mesh in the game; the fog cloak of [[Trainer Props]] as a fifth look.
