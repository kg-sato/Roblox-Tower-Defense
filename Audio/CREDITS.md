# Music credits and licences

All five tracks are **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)** according to their own OpenGameArt pages (checked 2026-10-25). CC0 does not require attribution; we credit the authors anyway. Files in `Music/raw/` are the untouched downloads; `Music/game/` are our leveled Ogg Vorbis versions (gain to a common loudness, soft limiter so the peak stays at or below -1 dBFS, 40 ms fade-in, 200 ms fade-out, 128 kbps). No other edits.

## Before publishing
- These files play in Studio only from a local folder (`content/sounds/music/`). To work for other players they must be **uploaded to Roblox from the owner's account**. That needs the owner's OK, and Roblox's audio checks may reject an upload; CC0 is a good licence, but acceptance on Roblox is not guaranteed and I could not find an official Roblox statement on Creative Commons audio.
- Put the author names in the game's credits screen (suggested text below).

```
Music (CC0): Gichco "Shinsei"; omfgdude "Chill lofi inspired"; RandomMind "Fantasy: Rising Moon"; Fupi "Artifact Room"; beardalaxy "Iremos Forest Theme Loop". Via OpenGameArt.org.
```
