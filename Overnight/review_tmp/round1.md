3 BIGGEST REMAINING PROBLEMS
1. UI has no material identity. Gameplay panels and buttons are flat dark fills with hairline gold borders and tiny thin serif text. The Victorian-steampunk theme exists only in the backdrop art of the reveal. Nothing has brass, glass or leather detail like shipped games (e.g. Bloons, Kingdom Rush, Genshin menus).
2. Readability and hierarchy. Most secondary text (HUD labels, odds, rarity, lore, stat labels) is far too small and low contrast; primary actions (BEGIN, ROLL, Start stage) are not prominent; the wave screen gives no combat feedback.
3. Inconsistent, unfinished feel. Mixed icon styles, mismatched colours (orange and purple versus brass), clipped or empty elements (empty results panel, bracket placeholder text, empty badge rings, cropped grid), HUD ghosting through modals, plus a creature model far cheaper than the generated backdrop.
