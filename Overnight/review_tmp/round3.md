3 BIGGEST REMAINING PROBLEMS
1. HUD is the weakest and most-seen screen: dark, tiny labels, no combat feedback (wave progress, unit readability, damage), scattered left rail with orphaned MENU/FAMILIARS, hollow stage ring. Needs a rebuilt layout, larger type, and unit/tower silhouettes.
2. No material or lighting on UI chrome: flat dark panels and thin gold lines across collection and results; zero texture, bevel, glow. Reads as a template, not a crafted Victorian-brass theme. Only the reveal's watch has real craft.
3. Inconsistent asset style: AI-painted backdrop, flat grey 3D wraith, mixed icon sets (line vs filled), differing character renders in the collection, plus tiny lore and label text. Needs one art direction, unified rarity colours, and minimum readable text size.

Overall verdict: FAIL for top-tier standard; reveal is closest (about 6/10 average), HUD and results are weakest.
