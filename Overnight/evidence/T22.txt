PASS effect budget and Low fallback implemented and measured in Play.
Cap: GameClient burst() keeps at most FXK.CAP live burst emitters (Low 10, Default 20, High 32; boss defeat and ultimate get +6 headroom), counts scale 0.5 / 1 / 1.25, lifetimes stay 0.4 to 0.9 s (inside 0.2 to 1 s), skipped bursts counted in FXK.skipped. Rate scaling: every world emitter with Rate > 0 gets Rate x 0.5 (Low) / 1 / 1.25 (High) via FXK.budgetEmitter (also for emitters added later). Secondary layers: on Low the 2nd and later emitter on a part is disabled; ProjectileFX star style drops its sparkle emitter and 3 of 5 rays on Low and raises Rate to 100 on High (VFX/ProjectileFX.luau, ProjectileFX.quality). AbilityFX now receives the player's particles quality.
Measured (6 Star Drake familiars fighting, same moment, setting toggled): sum of emitter Rate default 51.8 -> low 18.9 -> high 86.0 (about -63% on Low); familiar emitters enabled 9 (default) -> 3 (low) -> 9 (default again).
Live ParticleEmitter counts, boss wave (stage 5, auto waves, 6 Star Drakes), sampled every 0.1 s: Default (before the world budget, cap code in) peak 33, mean-stage peaks s1 22 s2 26 s3 25 s4 30 s5 33; Low after the budget peak 26 (s1 23 s2 24 s3 23 s4 25 s5 26); idle baseline 19 (portal, fog, ambient). Cap values: 10 / 20 / 32 live bursts. Samples 257 and 272.
Caveat: runs are random and the Default boss run was taken before the world budget existed, so the 33 -> 26 drop mixes the Low setting with randomness; the controlled numbers are the Rate sum and the familiar emitter counts. High and Default boss-wave peaks with the final code were not re-run. Not measured: frame rate on a phone.
