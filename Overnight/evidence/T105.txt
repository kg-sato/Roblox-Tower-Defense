Built: Studio_Export/FamiliarMaterials.luau, installed as ReplicatedStorage.Shared.FamiliarMaterials. Eight recipes in order: brass (Metal, #C9A24B), bone (Limestone, #E8E0C8), glass (Glass, #BEE1EB, transparency 0.45), stone (Slate, #64687A), ember (Neon #FF7628 + orange point light + spark emitter), frost (Ice #9ED4F0 + sparkle emitter), ink (Glass #0C0A14), starDust (Neon #465AD2 + blue point light + sparkle emitter). FamiliarMaterials.apply(part, name) sets material, colour, reflectance and transparency, adds the declared light and emitter (named FM_Light and FM_Emitter, replaced on a second apply, never stacked) and returns them; an unknown name raises an error. Design note with a table of uses, rules and open decisions: Roblox Dev/Design/Material Library.md.
Test: Tests/FamiliarMaterials.test.luau, run in Studio Edit mode through a temp ModuleScript on a fresh clone of Shared: "PASS FamiliarMaterials: 69/69 checks ok": 8 recipes in the stated order; each applies its material, colour, reflectance and transparency to a part; light and emitter only where declared; a second apply does not stack extras; no two recipes share material and colour; lightest colour above 0.7 luminance (bone) and darkest below 0.05 (ink); an unknown recipe raises an error.
Visual check: 8 spheres and 8 blocks, one pair per recipe, on a grey slab in Studio (screenshots viewed). Brass, bone, glass, stone and frost show distinct, readable surfaces (Slate and Limestone have real texture, Glass is see-through, Ice is veined); ink is a dark glossy sphere; ember and starDust are flat saturated colour (Neon has no surface detail, they rely on their light and sparkles in motion, which were not recorded).
Failures during the run, fixed (not hidden): (1) first run 63/69: the reflectance and transparency check compared floats exactly and failed on float32 rounding (0.28 stored as 0.2800000011920929), so I compared with a tolerance of 1e-4; (2) the ink colour was #161422, luminance 0.084 against my own "darkest below 0.05" intent, so I darkened it to #0C0A14; (3) after seeing the swatches the ink block looked silvery and the brass flat, so I changed ink reflectance 0.45 to 0.18 and brass 0.28 to 0.35. The tuned values were re-applied in Studio (module caching in Edit mode needed a fresh clone) and re-tested; the test passes 69/69 with the final values.
