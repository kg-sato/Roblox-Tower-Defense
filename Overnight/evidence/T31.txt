PASS AnnoMotion fantasy RPG icons (CC0) cut into 48 PNGs with alpha.
The JPG sheet is only 1200x630 (icons about 30 px) on a dark background, so it was not used; the PSD's composite (6144x2048 RGBA, PIL reads it without any new dependency) already has a transparent background (70.6 % transparent pixels). tools/icon_cut.py finds each icon as a blob of alpha (small detached bits joined first), pads it square with a transparent margin, writes 256 px PNGs to UI Kit/art/icons_fantasy/ and a contact sheet.
Result: 48 icons (= the pack's 48 named layers), edges clean on the dark contact sheet (Overnight/captures/T31_fantasy_icons.jpg): potions, mana flasks, swords, axe, mace, hammer, bow, quiver, dagger, shields, crossbow, helmets, circlet, armours, robe, four gems, food, coal, logs, rope, gold, scroll, books, rune stone. Layer names did not map to the blobs (PIL gives no layer boxes here), so files are numbered fantasy_icon_01..48 in row order. Logged in VFX/ASSETS.md (CC0). Not uploaded and not used in any screen yet.
