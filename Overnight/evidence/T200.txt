PASS (documented: Roblox Dev/Design/Progression Rules.md, section "Pity"; tested in Tests/Rules.test.luau, Studio Edit mode)
Rule: after 19 rolls without Epic or better the 20th is forced Epic or Legendary; the skill tree can shorten the interval, never below 8. Test: 6 seeds x 300 rolls on a MatchSim with the grid cleared after each roll: 1,800 rolls, longest dry streak 19, never 20; part of "PASS Rules: 31 of 31 checks".
Limits: tests the default interval only (no skill-tree pity value); not a statistical test of the odds themselves (the odds sum and luck direction are checked separately).
