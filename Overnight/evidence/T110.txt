PASS (3 expressions for each of the 4 familiars with a drawn face, as concept sheets; not applied to the game textures; look UNVERIFIED by the user)
Choice made: "the 4 familiars with faces" is not defined in the notes; I took the four with a drawn creature face in the model: Ember, Frostling, Brass Hound, Gargoyle. The other eight have masks, hoods, helmets or a hollow face.
Built: tools/familiar_expressions.py (Pillow, numpy; Hunyuan3D venv). On the textured front still of each familiar the original eyes and mouth are covered with the local colour (feathered) and redrawn as HAPPY (idle: closed arcs, smile), FIERCE (attack: narrowed eyes, lowered inner brows, zigzag mouth with a fang) and HURT (damage: wide eyes with highlight, raised inner brows, tear, frown). Outputs in Roblox Dev/Assets/Characters/Expressions/: <Name>_expressions.png (reference plus 3, zoomed face and full figure), _expressions_sheet.png, _expressions_report.txt (copy in Overnight/evidence/T110_report.txt). Design note with usage and the texture path: Roblox Dev/Design/Expressions.md.
Machine check ("familiars: 4 problems: 0"): every expression differs from the reference and from the other two (mean absolute difference of the face crop on a 0 to 255 scale: smallest pair 3.5 Ember, 4.3 Frostling, 2.2 Brass Hound, 4.0 Gargoyle; limit 2.0).
Visual check: viewed the 4 by 4 sheet twice. First run: the hurt brows sloped like an angry face and the cover patches showed as light discs; I flipped the hurt brows (inner end higher) and feathered the covers, then re-viewed: happy reads as content, fierce as angry, hurt as sad.
Limits: painted on a 384 px render, not on the 1024 UV texture, so they cannot be dropped into the game; Frostling's mouth patch is a visible bluish smudge; the Brass Hound's mouth sits on the collar edge; Gargoyle's happy eyes read weakly; "3 expressions each" counts happy, fierce and hurt (the modelled face is the reference, not counted); nothing applied in Roblox, so how a texture swap looks in game is untested; the choice of which four is mine and open for the user.
