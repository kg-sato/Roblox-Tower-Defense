PASS spike ran (Play, client). Answer: NO. A world ParticleEmitter cannot show a FrameArt / EditableImage texture in this Studio build; world flipbooks need uploaded sheets (TODO Section B).
Method: AssetService:CreateEditableImage 256x256, four coloured discs written with WritePixelsBuffer (a 2x2 flipbook). ParticleEmitter.TextureContent = Content.fromObject(img) is accepted without error and reads back as Content{SourceType=Object, Object=EditableImage}, FlipbookLayout Grid2x2, Size 8 to 12, LightEmission 1, Rate 4 to 8. A second emitter got FrameArt.glow() content (also accepted).
Result (capture Overnight/captures/T18_editableimage_particles_beam.jpg): the same EditableImage shows correctly in a ScreenGui ImageLabel (four discs, left); a control emitter with rbxasset sparkles renders (white star); a neon marker shows where the probe is; both EditableImage emitters draw nothing. A Beam with the EditableImage as TextureContent draws as a plain white band (texture ignored); the plain control Beam draws cyan.
So: EditableImage works for UI (ImageLabel, as FrameArt already does) but not as a ParticleEmitter or Beam texture. Workarounds without upload: UI-space flipbooks (ImageLabel + ImageRectOffset, T16 UI player) and BillboardGui ImageLabels in the world (not tested here).
