PASS (eye design system for all 12 familiars, drawn and machine-checked; concept drawings, look UNVERIFIED by the user)
Built: tools/eye_system.py (Pillow, numpy; Hunyuan3D venv). Each familiar's eyes come from its three tone words and are described by parameters (shape, size, gap, lid, pupil, highlights, tilt, extras) and drawn on a plain face disc in its identity colour. Six rules (size reads age, spacing reads trust, lids read mood, highlights read warmth, shape reads kind of mind, one look per familiar) and a 12-row table are in Roblox Dev/Design/Eye Design System.md. Sheets in Roblox Dev/Assets/Characters/Eyes/: _eye_sheet.png (256 px), _eye_sheet_small.png (48 px), _eye_report.txt (copy in Overnight/evidence/T111_report.txt).
Checks ("familiars: 12 ... problems: 0"): every pair of familiars differs in at least 2 of the 8 parameters; every pair differs in the 48 px greyscale render (smallest mean difference 6.9 on a 0 to 255 scale, Gargoyle / Mirror Chorister; limit 6); the eyes cover at least 1.5 percent of the face disc at 48 px (the Mourner is deliberately eyeless: a veil seam and two lash marks).
Fixes: the first run failed one check (the Archivist's thin lens rings covered 1.0 percent at 48 px), so I made the lens eye larger and its rings thicker; I also tightened the eye gaps after viewing the first sheet (the wide-set eyes sat at the disc edge). Both re-viewed.
Visual check: viewed the 12-face sheet and the 48 px strip: all twelve read as different characters; Gargoyle and Mirror Chorister are the closest pair at 48 px.
Limits: the drawings are not on the model textures (only Ember, Frostling, Brass Hound and Gargoyle have drawn faces today); the mapping from tone words to shapes is my judgement; the "matches the bible" part rests on the tone words and Ember's earlier eye note (large, round, wide-set, one highlight), since the Character Bible has no eye rule text; colour-blind and animation (blinks) not covered.
