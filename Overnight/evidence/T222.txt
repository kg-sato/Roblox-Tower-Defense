PASS (documented: Roblox Dev/Design/Content Plans.md, section "Twenty quests")
10 daily quests and 10 story quests tied to the stage sets and the lore beats, with gem rewards sized against the economy (3 dailies about 60 gems; story quests about 300 gems and 2 skill points in total). Quest D8 depends on the lantern power (T211), which is not built.
Limits: design only, not implemented; the numbers are not tested against the economy.
