### Repacked for Roblox (T27, `tools/fx_ingest.py`, 2026-10-27 overnight)
Each zip's `.tga` is read in memory (nothing run or extracted), alpha from luminance, 8x8 with transparent margins, neighbouring frames blended, violet-gold-white gradient map with an 8-level posterise; a white `_mono` sheet for tinting. Licence CC0 (Unity Labs), source URLs in the table above.
Outputs in `flipbooks/`: `ul_candlesmoke01_loop`, `ul_cloud01_loop`, `ul_explosion00`, `ul_explosion01`, `ul_explosion02` (one-shots: start at the brightest frame, 4 charge-up frames, clean fade at the end), `ul_fireball01_loop` to `ul_fireball04_loop`, `ul_flame03_loop`, `ul_wispysmoke01_loop` to `ul_wispysmoke03_loop` (loops: last quarter cross-faded into the first). Note: FireBall01 and FireBall03 are clipped to white in the 8-bit TGA, so their coloured sheets are flat; the EXR has the detail but this OpenCV build cannot read EXR. Use FireBall02/04 or the `_mono` sheets for those.
